CHAPTER 3
                       EMBERFALL
                  ASHES THAT BREATHE
   Recommended Levels 4–5 • Starting Level 4 • Hybrid Milestone Advancement
Chapter Purpose. Emberfall reveals the scale and precision of Rabu Shin’s
environmental testing. The characters descend through a mining city built beneath
an active volcano, trace rhythmic heat surges to a buried lattice crucible, and
confront a Molten Chimera Spawn engineered to survive extreme thermal stress.
Characters should reach 5th level after destroying the crucible, preventing a
magma catastrophe, and recovering proof that the next trial is glacial.


                             Emberfall settlement map


Emberfall
Emberfall clings to the black slopes beneath Mount Cindervane, an active volcano
whose lava channels power the settlement’s forges and mines. The city survives
through ore, glass, obsidian, and stubborn discipline. Its streets are carved into
basalt terraces. Iron bridges span glowing channels. Bells mark tremors, shift
changes, and evacuation routes. Recently, those bells have begun sounding at
precise forty-seven-minute intervals. Lava changes course without earthquakes.
Stone walls swell and contract like lungs. Miners speak of a crystal beneath the
mountain that breathes fire.
                                 ARRIVAL AT EMBERFALL
   A red glow stains the horizon long before Emberfall comes into view. The road
   climbs through fields of cooled black stone while ash drifts like gray snow.
   Ahead, a city of iron roofs and basalt walls crouches beneath a burning
   mountain. Hammers ring from a hundred forges. Then the ground shifts
   beneath your boots—once, twice, and once again—three measured pulses, each
   exactly the same strength. Every worker in sight goes silent.


Starting the Chapter
 • Stonewake Evidence. A heat-blackened shipping seal and the phrase “Proceed
    with thermal strain” point directly toward Emberfall.
 • Merchant Passage. Captain Meryn Holt provides transport to the volcanic coast,
   where the party arrives during a mine evacuation.
 • Foreman’s Contract. Foreman Garric offers 150 gp to investigate the
    disappearance of a survey crew in the Third Descent.
 • Arcane Inquiry. A Lunareth scholar asks the party to retrieve a sample of the red
   crystalline inclusions forming in Emberfall steel.
 Chapter Flow: The chapter opens with investigation in town, followed by the Breathing
 Crystal incident, a spreading crystal fever, and a descent into the mines. The central
 dungeon can be run as five linked zones: mine entrance, collapsed galleries, magma
 chamber, living stone passage, and the lattice crucible. Optional quests fit between major
 parts or can supply allies and resources for the descent.


Keyed Locations in Emberfall
1. Ember Tavern — A crowded miners’ hall where shift crews trade rumors.
Foreman Garric uses a private back room as an emergency command post.
2. Ashgrip Inn — A stone lodging house protected by heat wards. Travelers,
merchants, and a frightened surviving miner can be found here.
3. The Steel Forge — A heavy industrial forge producing rails, tools, and armor. Its
master smith has isolated weapons contaminated by red crystal.


4. The Brass Anvil — Durn Blackhammer’s renowned workshop. The forge burns
even when its vents are closed, and a blade quenched here recently whispered in
an unknown voice.
5. Grimroots Apothecary — Apothecary Grim treats burns and lung sickness. He
is the first to recognize that crystal fever is transformation, not infection.
6. Moltenvein Mercantile — Veskan Thrice-Weighed buys ore and funds
expeditions. His ledgers reveal missing shipments of alchemical reagents.
7. Wicks’ Wonders — A cluttered shop of lamps, lenses, climbing gear, and mining
charms. Wicks can provide specialized equipment for the descent.
8. Mine Entrance — The guarded mouth of Emberfall’s primary mine. Rails vanish
into heated darkness beneath a carved emergency arch.
9. The Lower Shafts — A network of galleries distorted by lattice influence. Walls
curve into smooth ribs and ore veins pulse with heat.
10. The Lattice Crucible — A hidden chamber suspended above magma where
Rabu Shin’s device redirects heat and grows the Molten Chimera Spawn.

Important NPCs
Foreman Garric — A practical human veteran who values lives over ore but hides
how many miners have already disappeared. He speaks in clipped orders and
knows the mine layout.


Miner Elra Voss — The only survivor of the missing survey crew. She heard a
calm voice speaking through the stone: “Structural resilience acceptable.”
Durn Blackhammer — A proud dwarf master smith at the Brass Anvil. He
distrusts arcane theory but respects evidence and courage.
Apothecary Grim — A soot-stained herbalist with a dry manner. He can stabilize
crystal fever but cannot cure it while the crucible remains active.
Veskan Thrice-Weighed — A wealthy ore merchant who unknowingly sold
specialized reagents to a House Varin agent. He fears public blame more than
danger.
Korin Ashvein — A young tiefling surveyor who knows old ventilation passages
and can guide the party if convinced the mission is not suicidal.


Part 1: The Breathing Crystal
                                   THE MINE ALARM
   A siren shrieks from the mine district. Workers scatter as an ore cart bursts
   through the gate, rolling without anyone pushing it. Inside lies a crystal mass
   the size of a child, glowing red beneath a skin of black glass. It expands.
   Contracts. Expands again. Four shapes peel themselves from the stone around
   it—small winged things with molten eyes and mouths full of sparks.


Four Magma Mephit Krasis attack near the mine entrance. They resemble ordinary
magma mephits reinforced with black crystalline plates and acidic glands. Their
goal is not to kill indiscriminately; two defend the crystal while two drive
witnesses away.

Encounter Features
 • Ore Carts. A creature can use an action to release a cart. It travels 30 feet in a
   straight line; creatures in its path make a DC 13 Dexterity save or take 7 (2d6)
   bludgeoning damage and fall prone.
 • Steam Vent. At initiative count 20, one marked vent erupts. Creatures within 5
    feet make a DC 12 Dexterity save or take 4 (1d8) fire damage.
 • Breathing Crystal. AC 14, 20 hit points, immune to poison and psychic damage.
   When reduced to 0 hit points, it becomes inert rather than exploding.
 • Crowd Hazard. A failed ranged attack by 5 or more risks striking a fleeing
    worker unless the attacker has a clear elevated position.
Studying the Crystal. A DC 15 Arcana check identifies a repeating transmutation
cycle that draws heat from the ground and stores it in layered tissue. A DC 14
Investigation check reveals tool marks: the crystal was deliberately placed inside
the ore cart.
                                 A MESSAGE IN HEAT
   When the last creature falls, the crystal contracts one final time. A pattern of


   green lines flickers across its surface, forming words in a language none of you
   can hear but all of you understand: “Thermal intake stable. Begin distributed
   ignition.”


Part 2: Lava Imp Uprising
Within an hour, reports arrive from the Steel Forge and Brass Anvil. Small
elemental creatures are emerging from vents, stealing heated metal, and carrying
it toward the mine. This is a coordinated collection effort directed by the crucible.
                                 THE FORGE REVOLT
   The Brass Anvil’s doors bulge outward. Through the windows, tools whirl in a
   cyclone of sparks while fist-sized creatures leap between the rafters. One drags
   a white-hot sword by the blade, laughing in crackling bursts. Durn
   Blackhammer braces the door from inside and roars, “Either help me or find a
   safer city!”


Run this as a three-round objective encounter against three magma mephits and
two magma mephit Krasis. The party must rescue Durn, shut two fuel valves, and
prevent the creatures from carrying a red-steel ingot into the mine.
 • Rescue Durn. DC 13 Athletics to force the warped door or DC 13 thieves’ tools to
   release the jammed mechanism.
 • Shut a Valve. An action and DC 12 Athletics or Sleight of Hand. Each open valve
    causes 3 (1d6) fire damage to one random creature at initiative count 20.
 • Protect the Ingot. A Krasis carrying the ingot has speed 20 feet and cannot fly. If
    it reaches the mine entrance, the Molten Chimera Spawn begins the final battle
    with 15 temporary hit points.
 • Forge Collapse. After three rounds, any open valve causes a roof beam to fall.
    Creatures beneath it make a DC 13 Dexterity save or take 9 (2d8) bludgeoning
    damage.
Reward. Durn provides one +1 weapon on loan for the remainder of the chapter. If
the party saves the forge and ingot, he permanently gifts the weapon after the
crucible is destroyed.


Part 3: Crystal Fever
Workers exposed to the breathing crystal develop fever, hardened veins, and
painful sensitivity to heat. Apothecary Grim has converted his shop into a ward.
The condition worsens whenever the underground node pulses.
                                 GRIMROOTS WARD
   Six miners lie beneath damp sheets. Red light moves through their veins in


   slow, branching lines. One woman grips the cot hard enough to crack the
   wood. “It keeps asking me to open the door,” she whispers. “There’s a door
   under the mountain, and it knows I can hear it.”


The characters must identify the condition and stabilize the patients before
descending. Run a skill challenge requiring four successes before three failures.
Useful checks include Arcana DC 14, Medicine DC 13, Nature DC 14, Religion DC 15,
or appropriate healing magic.
• On Success. All patients stabilize. Grim gives the party three doses of Cinderbane
  Tonic. As a bonus action, a creature can drink a dose to gain resistance to fire
  damage for 10 minutes.
• On Failure. Two patients transform partially and attack using magma mephit
  statistics without flight. After the encounter, the remaining patients stabilize but
  the party receives only one tonic.
• Key Clue. The fever is not contagious. It is a remote adaptation response caused
  by the crucible’s pulses.
• Elra’s Memory. When stabilized, Miner Elra draws a precise map of the Third
  Descent and marks the wall that “opened like a mouth.”


Part 4: Into the Lower Mines
                                THE THIRD DESCENT
   The mine swallows the sound of the city. Rails descend through chambers lit by
   red fungus and cracks of molten light. Every forty-seven minutes, the tunnel
   tightens around you with a deep grinding groan. Dust falls upward for a
   heartbeat. Far below, something answers with a slow, wet breath.


Environmental Rules
• Extreme Heat. After each hour underground, a creature without protection
  makes a DC 12 Constitution save or gains one level of exhaustion. Cinderbane
  Tonic or fire resistance prevents this save.
• Unstable Ground. Dashing across fractured stone requires a DC 11 Acrobatics
  check. On a failure, the creature falls prone.
• Pulse Cycle. Every forty-seven minutes, the lattice crucible pulses. Until the party
   reaches it, each pulse causes one nearby wall, bridge, or lava channel to shift.
• Mine Navigation. Following Elra’s map avoids one random encounter. Without
  it, the group must succeed on a DC 14 Survival check or lose 30 minutes and
  face a hazard.

Zone A: The Collapsing Shaft
                                STONE THAT MOVES
   The tunnel ahead has folded inward. Support beams bend like softened wax,
   and a narrow ledge crosses a shaft glowing orange far below. Halfway across,
   the stone wall opens in a smooth circular seam. A hand reaches out from
   inside the mountain. Then another.


Three lattice-bound mining constructs emerge. Use animated armor statistics, but
describe them as suits of abandoned mining gear filled with heated crystal. The
shaft collapses in stages.
• At initiative count 20 in round 1, stones fall in a 10-foot square: DC 13 Dexterity
  save, 7 (2d6) bludgeoning damage.
• In round 2, one 10-foot section of ledge breaks away. A creature on it can use a
   reaction to make a DC 13 Dexterity save and move to an adjacent safe square.
• In round 3, the remaining supports fail unless a character spends an action and
   succeeds on a DC 14 Athletics, mason’s tools, or carpenter’s tools check.
• A creature that falls is caught on a lower scaffold after 20 feet and takes 7 (2d6)
  bludgeoning damage rather than falling into lava.
Discovery. Inside the constructs are metal tags belonging to the missing survey
crew. Each tag bears a new rune meaning “iteration.”


Zone B: The Magma Chamber
A broad cavern is divided by a river of magma. Two Magma Mephit Krasis and one
fire snake guard a chain bridge. The mephits use Acid Spit to weaken armored
characters while the fire snake blocks the bridge.
 Diplomatic Alternative: The fire snake is not controlled by the lattice and can be driven
 away by destroying a red crystal embedded near its nest. A DC 14 Arcana or Animal
 Handling check reveals its distress. Removing the crystal turns the snake against one
 mephit before it retreats.


Zone C: The Breathing Wall
                                  THE LIVING PASSAGE
   The tunnel ends at a wall of polished black stone. Veins of red light spread
   across it in the shape of ribs. The entire surface moves inward, then outward,
   carrying a gust of air that smells of iron and burned hair. A vertical seam
   appears down its center.


The wall is a transformed section of mountain rather than a creature. Opening it
requires three successes before two failures using Arcana DC 15, Athletics DC 16,
Investigation DC 14, or mining tools DC 13. Each failure releases a heat pulse
dealing 5 (1d10) fire damage to everyone within 15 feet. The password “Refinement
requires iteration,” learned in Greymoor Hollow, counts as one automatic success.


Part 5: The Lattice Crucible
                              THE MOLTEN CHIMERA SPAWN
   The passage opens above a chamber vast enough to hold a cathedral. A crystal
   structure rotates over a lake of magma, suspended by chains of light. Streams
   of fire rise against gravity and pour into its facets. Beneath it, something
   massive pulls itself from a basin of molten stone—three bodies forced into one:
   draconic jaws, a horned furnace-skull, and a leonine frame plated in obsidian.
   Every head opens its eyes at once.


The Crucible Chamber
• Central Crucible. AC 16, 50 hit points, immunity to fire, poison, and psychic
   damage; vulnerability to cold and thunder. It floats 15 feet above the magma
   basin.
• Suspension Chains. Three light-chains connect the crucible to stone pylons. A
   chain has AC 14 and 15 hit points. Destroying a chain lowers the crucible 5 feet
   and disables one pulse effect.


 • Magma Channels. A creature entering a channel takes 10 (3d6) fire damage.
   Narrow bridges provide access to each pylon.
 • Cooling Valves. Two ancient mining valves can be opened with an action and DC
    14 Athletics. Each opened valve deals 10 cold damage to the crucible and creates
    a cloud of steam that heavily obscures a 10-foot area until the next round.
 • Escape Tunnel. A reinforced passage leads upward. It becomes accessible when
   the crucible is destroyed.

Molten Chimera Tactics
 • The chimera opens with Furnace Breath if it can catch at least two characters.
 • It uses Pounce to cross bridges and isolate a character near a pylon.
 • If a character attacks the crucible, one head focuses exclusively on that
    character until another creature deals more damage to the chimera.
 • At half hit points, its obsidian plates crack. Its speed increases by 10 feet, but its
   Armor Class decreases by 1.
 • If the red-steel ingot reached the mine in Part 2, the creature begins with 15
    temporary hit points.
                                  RABU SHIN OBSERVES
     The three-headed creature roars, but a second voice speaks beneath the sound
     —calm, measured, almost pleased. “Thermal strain exceeds projections.
     External variables introduced. Continue observation.” The crucible turns, and
     for an instant a tall human silhouette appears inside the crystal.


Destroying the Crucible
When the crucible reaches 0 hit points, it fractures and releases its stored heat.
Each creature makes a DC 14 Dexterity save, taking 14 (4d6) fire damage on a
failure or half on a success. The Molten Chimera Spawn takes the damage
automatically and loses Adaptive Surge. If the chimera is already dead, the blast
begins the escape sequence immediately.

Escape from the Lower Mines
The party has six rounds to reach the reinforced passage before the chamber fills
with magma. Run the escape as three group checks: Athletics or Acrobatics DC 13,
Survival or Investigation DC 13, and Arcana or mining tools DC 14. On each failed
group check, every character takes 3 (1d6) fire or bludgeoning damage. A character
can remain behind for one round to grant the group advantage on the next check,
but must then make a DC 15 individual check to catch up.
                             THE MOUNTAIN EXHALES
   The mountain convulses as the crystal breaks. Lava crashes down into its
   natural channels, no longer climbing toward the machine. Behind you, the
   chamber folds inward in a storm of sparks and black stone. Ahead, a line of
   cool night air cuts through the smoke. When you emerge, every forge bell in
   Emberfall begins to ring—not in warning, but in celebration.


Optional Quests in Emberfall
Part 6: Ore Thieves
A gang has been stealing red-crystal ore from sealed storage. Four bandits and one
bandit captain operate from an abandoned smelter. They were hired by a masked
buyer from Silverreach. Their ledger records payments marked with House Varin’s
silver stag seal.
Reward. 60 gp, two alchemist’s fire, and evidence that later grants advantage on
one investigation check in Silverreach.

Part 7: Living Magma
A lava channel has developed purposeful movement and is approaching homes.
Use a fire elemental with half hit points, but allow the party to end the encounter
without destroying it by removing a lattice shard embedded in the channel wall.
Three successes before two failures using Arcana DC 14, Athletics DC 15, or smith’s
tools DC 13 calms the flow.

Part 8: Black Lung Plague
Miners in the Ashgrip Inn cough up soot that forms tiny geometric patterns. The
illness is caused by powdered lattice residue in ventilation ducts. Cleaning the
ducts requires navigating roof vents while two smoke mephits attack. Grim
rewards the party with two greater antitoxin doses, each granting advantage on
one Constitution saving throw against poison or disease.

Part 9: The Shattered Vein
A newly exposed ore vein sings when struck. Durn wants a sample; Lunareth
agents want it destroyed. The vein contains encoded research notes rather than
raw power. A DC 16 Arcana check extracts the message: “Photosynthetic host
integration scheduled.” This points toward Lunareth and Chapter 4.

Part 10: Ashbound Spirit
The ghost of a miner named Talla appears near the Third Descent. She cannot rest
because her crew’s bodies were incorporated into lattice constructs. Recovering
their tags and performing a burial rite prevents one random mine hazard and
grants each character inspiration before the final descent.


Chapter Resolution
Rewards
 • Foreman Garric pays 150 gp for ending the threat and rescuing any surviving
    miners.


• Durn Blackhammer permanently grants the loaned +1 weapon if the Brass Anvil
  and red-steel ingot were saved.
• Apothecary Grim supplies two potions of fire resistance for future travel.
• The party can recover a Thermal Lattice Fragment, which functions as a
   required key in Sunspire later in the campaign.
• House Varin evidence from Ore Thieves strengthens the political investigation in
  Silverreach.

Thermal Lattice Fragment
Wondrous item, story item
This palm-sized piece of red-black crystal remains warm in any climate. Once per
long rest, its bearer can use a reaction when taking fire damage to reduce that
damage by 1d10 + the bearer’s proficiency bonus. The fragment is also one of the
keys needed to enter the Gene Vault at Sunspire.

Milestone Advancement
Characters advance to 5th level when they destroy the lattice crucible, defeat or
neutralize the Molten Chimera Spawn, and prevent a catastrophic magma surge
from reaching Emberfall. If they solve the crisis without combat, award the
milestone when the thermal experiment is permanently disabled and its purpose is
understood.

What the Characters Learn
• Rabu Shin’s devices are climate-specific experiments built to measure controlled
  adaptation.
• The lattice network can reshape terrain as well as living creatures.
• Rabu Shin can observe events remotely through major nodes.
• House Varin or its agents are purchasing and transporting research materials.
• The next stage is called “glacial calibration,” pointing toward Frostharbor.
• The Shattered Vein message also implicates Lunareth’s living forest research.
                                     ASHES SETTLE
   At dawn, Mount Cindervane still burns—but its rhythm belongs to the earth
   again. Miners return to the upper galleries, and smoke rises from repaired
   forges. In the shattered crucible, the party finds a thin crystal plate etched with
   two lines: “Thermal strain viable. Structural resilience acceptable. Proceed to
   glacial phase.” Beneath the words is a map coordinate far to the north—and
   the initials R.S.
