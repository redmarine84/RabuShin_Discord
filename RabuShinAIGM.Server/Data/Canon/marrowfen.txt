CHAPTER 6
                     MARROWFEN
                 THE HEART OF THE FEN
   Recommended Levels 7-8 • Starting Level 7 • Hybrid Milestone Advancement
Chapter Purpose. Marrowfen reveals that Rabu Shin’s operation has advanced
beyond isolated experiments. Beneath the marsh lies an automated production
laboratory and a relay core transmitting results to Aetherfall. The characters must
navigate a failing ecosystem, uncover a submerged facility, survive amphibious
Krasis prototypes, and destroy the Swamp Hydra Krasis before the fen is converted
into a breeding basin. Characters should reach 8th level after collapsing the
laboratory and securing its relay data.


                             Marrowfen settlement map


Marrowfen
Marrowfen is a stubborn settlement raised on stilts above black water. Narrow
boardwalks link crooked homes, smokehouses, shrines, and docks. The people
know every current and every patch of safe ground. Lately, however, familiar
waterways have begun moving against the wind, entire pools have drained
overnight, and the swamp’s usual rot has been replaced by a sterile metallic odor.
                                ARRIVAL IN MARROWFEN
   The road dissolves into a plank walkway above endless reeds and black water.
   Lanterns burn as dim yellow smudges in the mist. No frogs call. No insects
   hum. Beneath the boards, a ripple travels against the current and stops
   directly below you. A fisherman watches from a leaning porch. “It ain’t the
   swamp that’s wrong,” he mutters. “It’s what’s under it.”


Starting the Chapter
 • The Amphibious Relay. The Krasis Commander’s journal identifies a major
    active relay in marsh terrain southeast of High Bastion.
 • A Plea from the Fen Watch. Ranger Nessa Reed arrives with reports of geometric
   drainage channels and missing patrols.
 • Trade Contamination. Stonewake merchants report barrels of Marrowfen water
    that crystallize when exposed to moonlight.
 • The Varin Manifest. A Silverreach shipping record lists pumps, glass tanks, and
    alchemical preservatives delivered to Marrowfen under a false charity seal.
 Chapter Flow: The party gathers rumors in Marrowfen, investigates artificial drainage
 and disappearances, and locates a half-submerged laboratory during the lowest tide. The
 laboratory becomes a compact dungeon featuring containment chambers, pumping
 machinery, hybrid creatures, and a transmitting relay. The finale pits the party against
 the Swamp Hydra Krasis while the facility floods and collapses.


Keyed Locations in Marrowfen
1. The Bog Lantern Inn. A crowded stilt-house inn run by Sella Mirewick. Its
common room serves as tavern, meeting hall, and emergency shelter.
2. Fen Watch Tower. A raised ranger station overlooking the widest channels.
Maps show water loss in precise geometric wedges.
3. Mire Dock. Flat-bottomed skiffs leave from this sagging pier. Boats have begun
scraping against metal structures beneath the mud.
4. Sunken Chapel. An old stone shrine exposed by falling water levels. Its altar
bears lattice-like spiral carvings.
5. Crooked Reed Market. A floating market of herb sellers, fishers, and trappers.
Several stalls display pale fish with reinforced gills.


6. The Sinking Basin. A broad patch of marsh collapsing around a newly exposed
cylindrical structure.
7. The Submerged Laboratory. A sealed Krasis facility containing pumps, vats,
specimen pens, and the relay core.
8. The Blackwater Spillway. A drainage trench that carries contaminated water
toward the open fen.

Part 1: The Fen That Drains
                               THE ARTIFICIAL CHANNELS
   Beyond the last house, the marsh opens into a field of cracked mud. Reeds lie
   flat in rows as straight as spear shafts. Water trickles into narrow cuts that
   should not exist, all flowing toward a dark depression in the distance. Every
   forty-seven heartbeats, the channels pulse and deepen.


Nessa asks the characters to survey three drainage sites. Treat this as a 4-success-
before-2-failure skill challenge. Useful skills include Investigation to identify tool
marks, Survival to trace hidden channels, Nature to understand the damaged
ecosystem, Arcana to sense lattice resonance, and Athletics to cross collapsing
mud. A failure causes a character to sink waist-deep and take 1d6 acid damage
from contaminated water.
 • Success identifies the Sinking Basin as the convergence point and reveals that
    the pumps activate during the lowest tide.
 • One failure alerts two Flesh-Grafted Crocodiles, which stalk the party during the
   next investigation scene.
 • Two failures cause a boardwalk collapse in town, injuring civilians and reducing
    local confidence in the party.

Fenlight Disappearances
Three residents vanished after pursuing pale green lights. Following the lights
leads to a shallow pool where an Ooze-Ghoul Hybrid imitates drowned voices. A
DC 15 Insight check notices that each plea repeats with identical pauses. Defeating
the hybrid reveals stitched identification tags from the missing residents and a
brass token marked with a laboratory chamber number.

The Sunken Chapel
                                  THE EXPOSED SHRINE
   Mud covers the chapel floor in a smooth black sheet. At the far end, a stone
   altar rises above the water. Old devotional carvings spiral around its sides, but
   newer cuts interrupt them: sharp, precise angles glowing faintly green. The bell
   above you rings once. No wind moves.


Brother Cale explains that the shrine was built atop something older. Investigation
DC 15 uncovers a metal conduit behind the altar. Arcana DC 16 identifies it as a
signal repeater connected to the laboratory. Destroying or disabling it prevents the
facility from tracking the party’s approach and removes one environmental pulse
from the final battle.
 A Choice, Not a Puzzle: The altar is sacred to Marrowfen. Smashing it is fast but
 damages the community’s trust. Careful removal requires one hour and a successful DC


 15 thieves’ tools or mason’s tools check.


Part 2: The Sinking Basin
                               THE LABORATORY EMERGES
   Mist parts over a widening sinkhole. Black water circles a cylinder of stone,
   iron, and pale tissue rising from the mud. Pipes flex like roots along its sides,
   drinking from the fen. A round hatch sits ten feet above the waterline.
   Something large rolls beneath the surface, leaving three wakes where one body
   should be.


The laboratory can be entered through the upper hatch, a cracked pump conduit,
or a submerged maintenance grate. Each route changes the first encounter.

Perimeter Encounter
Two Flesh-Grafted Crocodiles guard the basin. They remain submerged until a
creature reaches the tower or enters the water. One attempts to grapple and tow
prey toward an intake pipe. Characters who observed their tracks earlier have
advantage on initiative. If reduced below half hit points, a crocodile retreats
through the maintenance grate and warns the laboratory.


Part 3: The Submerged Laboratory
The interior is dry, warm, and unnaturally clean. Pale tubes pulse behind glass
walls. Brass plates identify chambers in precise Elvish script. Unless noted
otherwise, ceilings are 12 feet high, doors are watertight, and dim green crystals
provide light.

1. Intake Gallery
Three pumps pull water and organic matter from the fen. A character can disable
each pump with a DC 15 Arcana, Athletics, or thieves’ tools check. Each disabled
pump slows flooding during the boss battle and restores part of the marsh after the
chapter.

2. Specimen Receiving
Hooks, cages, and numbered tables hold captured swamp life. Two giant frogs and
a restrained young crocodile can be freed. A ledger shows that subjects were
selected for lung capacity, toxin resistance, and regenerative response.

3. Production Chamber
Six glass vats contain incomplete hybrids. Two Ooze-Ghoul Hybrids emerge when
the central console is touched. Breaking a vat creates a 10-foot pool of corrosive
slime that deals 2d6 acid damage to a creature entering it for the first time on a
turn.

4. Pump Control Walkway
A narrow bridge crosses churning machinery. At initiative count 20, unsecured
creatures make a DC 13 Dexterity save or slide 10 feet toward the gears. Shutting
down the machinery removes this hazard and weakens the final relay pulse.

5. Surgical Archive
Crystal slates record successful grafts. Medicine DC 15 reveals that many subjects
were alive and conscious. One file bears House Varin authorization codes and
another references “surface stress testing.”

6. Flooded Containment
Waist-deep water conceals a bound Water Weird and the remains of failed
specimens. Destroying the binding pillar frees the weird, which attacks the nearest
creature unless calmed with Primordial or a DC 16 Animal Handling check
enhanced by magic.


7. Relay Antechamber
Three rotating rings project maps of Emberfall, Lunareth, High Bastion, and
Frostharbor. Arcana DC 18 confirms that the laboratory is transmitting data rather
than merely collecting it.

8. Heart Chamber
The Swamp Hydra Krasis rests around a crystalline relay core suspended over
black water. Its necks are threaded through the machinery like living cables.
 Resting in the Laboratory: A short rest is possible after the production chamber if the
 party seals both doors. A long rest allows the relay to finish a transmission, granting the
 Swamp Hydra Krasis 20 temporary hit points and causing Rabu Shin to learn one
 character’s damage resistances.


Optional Marrowfen Quests
The Bog Hag’s Bargain
A reclusive green hag named Mother Brindle offers a vial that suppresses Krasis
regeneration for 1 minute. Her price is a cherished memory willingly described by
one character. She does not steal the memory; she wants the story and later
repeats it to unsettle the character. Refusing her bargain has no mechanical
penalty.

Mire Rot
Contaminated water causes fever and blackened veins among residents. Velka
needs three ingredients gathered from dangerous locations. Completing the task
grants four antitoxin doses and prevents civilian deaths during the laboratory
collapse.

Floating Bones
Human bones arranged in geometric rafts drift through the eastern channel. The
remains belong to laboratory victims. Returning their identification tags to families
earns Marrowfen’s trust and reveals that one victim worked for a Silverreach
shipping house.

Fenfire Beacon
A watch beacon has been replaced by a lattice transmitter. Disabling it before
entering the laboratory prevents reinforcements: otherwise one additional Ooze-
Ghoul Hybrid arrives during the final battle on round 3.

The White-Eyed Gar
A huge mutated fish circles the market docks. It is frightened rather than
aggressive. Calming and relocating it requires a 3-success skill challenge using
Animal Handling, Nature, Survival, or magic. Success earns a charm from local
fishers that allows one character to breathe underwater for 1 hour.


Part 4: The Heart of the Fen
                                THE SWAMP HYDRA KRASIS
     The final door opens onto a cavernous chamber filled with black water. A
     crystal relay turns above the pool, shedding green light across dozens of tubes.
     One by one, massive heads rise from the surface. Their jaws are crocodilian,
     their eyes human, and translucent glands pulse along their throats. The central
     head opens its mouth, and Rabu Shin’s voice speaks through it: “Amphibious
     strain viable. Begin surface stress testing.”


Battlefield Features
 • Black Water. The pool is 4 feet deep and difficult terrain for creatures without a
   swim speed.
 • Relay Core. AC 17, 45 hit points, immune poison and psychic. Destroying it ends
   relay pulses but causes the chamber to begin collapsing.
 • Pump Columns. Each intact pump column grants the hydra 5 additional hit
    points whenever its Bog Regeneration activates. A column has AC 15 and 20 hit
    points.
 • Suspended Walkways. A creature shoved from a walkway falls 10 feet into the
    pool and lands prone.
 • Containment Valves. A character can open a valve with an action and DC 15
    Athletics check, creating a 20-foot line of rushing water that pushes creatures 15
    feet.

Hydra Tactics
The Swamp Hydra Krasis begins by spreading its heads across several targets. It
uses Devouring Drag on isolated characters and positions itself near pump
columns. When reduced below half hit points, it ruptures its acid glands, making
the water lightly obscured and hazardous. It understands the relay core’s
importance and uses a reaction to intercept attacks against it when possible.

Collapsing the Laboratory
When the hydra and relay core are destroyed, the facility begins to collapse. The
party has 6 rounds to escape. Each round, one character must lead the group
through a hazard using Athletics, Acrobatics, Survival, Arcana, or tools. Three total
successes allow everyone to escape safely. Each failure deals 2d6 damage to every
character and costs one recovered item or rescued creature.
                                 THE HEART BREAKS
   The relay fractures with a sound like a glass cathedral breaking underwater.
   Green light races through every tube. The walls convulse. Beyond the chamber,
   swamp water punches through the corridors in a roaring black wave.
   Somewhere above, the whole tower begins to sink.


Aftermath
With the pumps destroyed, water returns gradually to the drained channels. Fish
and insects reappear over the following weeks, though scars remain around the
Sinking Basin. The recovered relay data proves that the Krasis network is
automated, distributed, and linked to Frostharbor and Aetherfall.
                             WHAT THE RELAY REVEALS
   At dawn, the last piece of the laboratory slips beneath the recovering marsh.
   Sella watches the water cover it and exhales. Nessa holds up a crystal slate
   still pulsing with coordinates. “This thing was talking to somewhere,” she says.
   Two points burn on the display: one beneath the northern ice, and one
   impossibly high above the world.


Rewards
 • Marrowfen grants the party 900 gp gathered from trade guilds and grateful
   families.
 • Velka provides four greater healing potions if Mire Rot was completed, or two
   otherwise.


• The party recovers a Relay Crystal keyed to Aetherfall. Once, it can grant
   advantage on all Arcana checks made during one encounter involving a lattice
   device.
• Hydra acid glands yield up to three doses of potent acid, each dealing 4d6 acid
  damage when thrown as an improvised weapon.
• Each character receives the Fen-Walker Token, granting advantage on one
  Survival check made in swamp terrain.

Milestone Advancement
Characters advance to 8th level after destroying the relay core, defeating the
Swamp Hydra Krasis, and preventing the laboratory from converting Marrowfen
into a permanent production basin.

If the Laboratory Survives
• The marsh continues draining and becomes a sterile basin filled with breeding
   vats.
• Trade between Stonewake and the eastern settlements collapses.
• Rabu Shin deploys amphibious Krasis in a later chapter.
• The final Aetherfall encounter begins with one additional active relay node.

Transition to Silverreach
                                THE MONEY TRAIL
  Among the recovered shipping manifests is a sequence of payments bearing the
  crest of House Varin. Pumps, preservatives, living specimens, and guards were
  purchased through charitable accounts in Silverreach. The final entry reads:
  “Urban integration funding approved.” Marrowfen was not the destination. It
  was the supply chain.
