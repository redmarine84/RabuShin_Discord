Chapter 9
                    FROSTHARBOR
                        FROZEN EVOLUTION
                Recommended Level 9 • Advancement to Level 10

 DM NOTE. Chapter Purpose. Frostharbor is the culmination of Rabu Shin’s
 environmental calibration program. The characters discover that the northern
 experiments are no longer trials but final synchronization tests. By destroying the glacial
 lattice core, they gain the coordinates and key needed to reach Sunspire and learn that
 Rabu Shin has transferred his consciousness beyond a single body.


                                Frostharbor settlement map


Frostharbor
Frostharbor rests where black cliffs meet the frozen northern sea. Its homes are
built from dark timber, whale bone, and stone quarried from beneath the ice.
Fishing vessels spend most of the year locked in the harbor, and the people survive
through seal hunting, ice mining, and stubborn preparation. Every roof is steep,
every doorway is reinforced, and every resident knows how quickly warmth can
become life or death.


For the past month, the ice has behaved with impossible precision. Cracks form in
repeating geometric patterns. Frozen ships rise from beneath the harbor intact.
Hunting beasts move in coordinated packs, and a pale green light flashes beneath
the glacier shelf during storms. The entire region is being used as the final cold-
resistance test for the Krasis lattice network.

  ARRIVAL IN FROSTHARBOR
  The northern wind strikes like a blade. Snow races sideways across the road, and
  the harbor beyond lies trapped beneath a white plain of ice. Ship masts protrude
  from the frozen sea like the ribs of a buried beast. Then a deep crack rolls
  through the harbor. The sound is too even, too measured. A line of green light
  travels beneath the ice, turns at a perfect angle, and vanishes beneath the distant
  glacier.


Starting the Chapter
The party reaches Frostharbor with the access cipher recovered in Duskmire
Crossing and evidence that Doctor Cael Veyr shipped activation compounds north.
The cipher identifies a receiving station hidden beneath the Lighthouse of the Pale
Beam. Captain Elska Rimeborn meets the party at the southern gate and
immediately asks for help: three hunters vanished near a newly opened fissure,
and the lighthouse has not shown its true white signal in four nights.
• The Frost Cipher. The code recovered from Cael opens sealed doors within the
   glacial research vault.
• Missing Hunters. Families offer 600 gp for the return of the glacier scouts, dead
  or alive.
• The Green Lighthouse. Harbor pilots believe the altered beacon is drawing
   creatures toward the town.
• The Frozen Emissary. House Varin records identify an unnamed “northern
   emissary” who completed the glacial phase and reported directly to Rabu Shin.

Extreme Cold
Outside heated structures, a creature exposed to the weather for 1 hour must
succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or gain one level of exhaustion. Cold-
weather clothing grants advantage. A creature with resistance or immunity to cold
damage automatically succeeds. During whiteouts, visibility is limited to 60 feet
and ranged weapon attacks beyond 30 feet are made with disadvantage.

 DM NOTE. Do not use the weather only as attrition. Warm shelters, local guides, magical
 fire, and good planning should let the characters control the risk. The cold should create
 urgency and atmosphere rather than simply consume resources.


Keyed Locations in Frostharbor
1. Frost Winds Inn. A crowded inn built around a communal stone hearth.
Proprietor Torv Blackhelm knows every hunter and sailor in town and keeps a
board of missing names above the fireplace.
2. The Winter Hearth. A public hall, shrine, and emergency shelter overseen by
Sister Ylsa. The hearth flame has recently begun leaning toward the glacier even
when no doors are open.
3. Frozen Docks. Ships sit locked in ice. Dockworkers have found old vessels rising
from below, their hulls preserved but their interiors stripped clean.
4. Icebound Tavern. Sailors gather here to trade rumors. A survivor named Varek
claims something beneath the ice spoke through his teeth.
5. Compass Rose Market. Fur traders, ice miners, and shipwrights sell cold-weather
gear. Several merchants unknowingly received green-glass heating stones seeded
with lattice fragments.
6. Brightmane Apothecary. Healer Lysa Brightmane treats frostbite and strange
crystalline lesions. She can identify the difference between natural cold injury and
biomantic reinforcement.
7. Lighthouse of the Pale Beam. The beacon stands on a rocky promontory. Its
crystal has been modified to broadcast lattice pulses across the harbor.
8. Glacier Shelf. A newly opened fissure leads to the buried Glacial Vault, where the
final cold-calibration core is preparing to transmit its results to Aetherfall.

Part 1: The Harbor That Cracks Inward
  THE FROZEN DOCKS
  The dock planks disappear beneath wind-polished ice. Sailors work with axes
  and heated iron, but every trench they cut closes behind them in smooth, glassy
  sheets. Farther out, a dark hull rises slowly through the frozen harbor. No ropes
  pull it. No current moves it. The ice simply lifts the ship into the world.


The newly surfaced vessel is the Northstar, lost forty-two years ago. Its hull is
intact, but every bunk and storage locker has been emptied. Investigation DC 15
reveals no sign of decay inside. Arcana DC 16 identifies a preservation field linked
to a pulse beneath the harbor. A set of fresh boot prints leads from the vessel
toward the lighthouse, though the ship was under solid ice until an hour ago.

Encounter: Predators Beneath the Ice
While the party investigates, two Winter Wolves burst through weakened ice and
attack. They fight with unnatural coordination, attempting to drive characters onto
marked sections of the harbor. At initiative count 20, one marked 10-foot square
fractures. A creature standing there must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving
throw or fall into freezing water.
A creature in the water takes 7 (2d6) cold damage at the start of each turn.
Climbing out requires 15 feet of movement and a successful DC 12 Athletics check
unless aided. After the battle, Nature or Arcana DC 15 reveals tiny green crystal
slivers embedded behind each wolf’s ears. The slivers transmit commands from
the lighthouse.

The Raised Ship
The Northstar’s captain’s cabin contains a frost-sealed journal. Warming it slowly
reveals the final entry: “The light changed. The crew followed it north across the
ice. I stayed because I heard another voice beneath it.” A hidden compartment
contains a silver navigational disk worth 250 gp and a map showing an older
tunnel beneath the lighthouse.


Part 2: The Pale Beam
The Lighthouse of the Pale Beam can be approached openly, by scaling the icy cliff,
or through the flooded maintenance tunnel shown on the Northstar map. Keeper
Orin Vale has barricaded himself in the lantern room. Three Lattice Thralls guard
the lower floors, using Veteran statistics with resistance to cold damage and green
crystalline scarring.


Lighthouse Areas
1. Entry Hall. The inner walls are coated with frost despite a burning stove. A DC 14
Investigation check finds drag marks leading toward the cellar.
2. Keeper’s Quarters. Letters reveal that Orin’s daughter was abducted by the
glacial overseer. Orin obeyed to keep her alive.
3. Maintenance Cellar. A lattice amplifier has been installed around the original
beacon conduit. Destroying it requires three successful DC 15 Arcana, Athletics, or
thieves’ tools checks before two failures. Each failure releases a pulse dealing 7
(2d6) lightning damage to the acting character.
4. Spiral Stair. Crystalline ice grows across the steps. Characters moving faster than
half speed must succeed on a DC 13 Acrobatics check or fall prone and slide 20 feet.
5. Lantern Room. The original white beacon crystal is encased in green lattice
growth. Orin stands beside it holding a flare pistol, terrified and desperate.

  THE ALTERED LIGHT
  The beacon turns above you, but its light is no longer white. A narrow green
  beam sweeps across the frozen sea, and each time it passes, distant cracks
  answer like drums. Keeper Orin grips the brass railing with bloodless fingers. “I
  tried to stop it,” he says. “It knows when I lie.”


Saving Keeper Orin
Orin is not hostile unless attacked. Persuasion DC 15, presenting evidence of the
party’s victories, or promising to rescue his daughter convinces him to help. He
explains that a pale mage called Vaelgor entered through a fissure in the glacier
and used the lighthouse to synchronize every lattice fragment in the region. Orin’s
daughter, Tessa, was taken into the vault as leverage.
If the party destroys or purifies the amplifier, the command pulses stop. All
characters gain inspiration, and Captain Rimeborn can send four scouts to support
one later encounter. The beacon crystal also yields a Pale Prism, which can
suppress one environmental pulse in the glacial vault as a reaction.


Part 3: Frozen Side Quests
The following quests can be completed before the party enters the glacier. Each
provides information, allies, or resources for the final assault.

The White-Eyed Seal
A seal with luminous white eyes repeatedly approaches the docks carrying scraps
of red cloth. Following it leads to a trapped hunter beneath a shelf of ice. Rescuing
the hunter requires a DC 14 group Athletics or Survival check. Success reveals the


location of a safe crevasse entrance and grants advantage on the first navigation
check in the glacier.

The Frozen Fishing Fleet
Three fishing boats are being dragged beneath the ice by a Water Weird altered to
deal cold instead of bludgeoning damage. Freeing the boats earns 400 gp in trade
goods and the loyalty of the harbor crews. Once during the final escape, those
crews can provide ropes and anchors that convert one failed group check into a
success.

Black Ice Contamination
Children collecting heating stones have fallen ill. The stones contain dormant
lattice splinters. Locating and destroying the shipment requires Investigation DC 15
and a confrontation with four smugglers using Bandit statistics. Lysa Brightmane
rewards the party with four potions of cold resistance.

The Icebound Relic
Sister Ylsa asks the characters to recover the Sunsteel Brazier from a shrine
swallowed by the glacier. The shrine is guarded by two Ice Mephits and a Helmed
Horror. Returning the brazier allows the Winter Hearth to bless the party. Each
character gains 10 temporary hit points upon entering the Glacial Vault.

The Missing Hunters
The hunters are trapped in an abandoned ice-mining camp where a Polar Wyvern
Krasis circles overhead. The creature retreats toward the glacier if reduced below
half its hit points. Saving the hunters provides a detailed sketch of the vault
entrance and confirms that Tessa Vale is alive inside.


Part 4: Crossing the Glacier
  THE GLACIER SHELF
  The glacier rises before you like a frozen mountain range. Across its face, cracks
  form a perfect spiral hundreds of feet wide. At the spiral’s center, a vertical
  fissure glows with pale green light. Snow falls toward it instead of down,
  vanishing into the opening as if the ice itself were inhaling.


Reaching the fissure requires three group checks. The party must earn two
successes before two failures. Useful skills include Survival DC 15 to choose a
route, Athletics DC 14 to cross a crevasse, Perception DC 15 to spot unstable ice, and
Arcana DC 16 to predict lattice pulses. A failure deals 10 (3d6) cold damage to each
character and alerts the vault guardians.


Encounter: Polar Wyvern Krasis
At the final ridge, a Polar Wyvern Krasis attacks from a whiteout. It uses the storm
to isolate one target, then attempts to drag that creature toward a crevasse. If the
party previously rescued the hunters, they receive warning and cannot be
surprised. The creature bears a metal collar engraved with the words “GLACIAL
STRAIN: VIABLE.”


Part 5: The Glacial Vault
The vault is an ancient research complex encased in blue ice and reinforced with
black metal. Green crystalline conduits run through the walls like veins. Unless the
lighthouse amplifier was destroyed, Vaelgor knows the party’s exact location and
all vault encounters begin with enemies prepared.

Vault Features
• Ceilings are 20 feet high unless noted otherwise.
• Walls are magically reinforced ice with AC 17, 30 hit points per 10-foot section,
  immunity to cold and psychic damage, and vulnerability to fire damage.
• Bright green conduits provide dim light. Destroying a conduit section ends one
   nearby environmental effect but deals 5 (1d10) force damage to creatures within
   5 feet.
• At initiative count 20 during major encounters, the core emits a Glacial Pulse
  unless the party uses the Pale Prism or has disabled the associated relay.

1. Calibration Gate
The Frost Cipher from Duskmire opens the circular gate without triggering its
defenses. Without the cipher, the party must succeed on three DC 17 Arcana or
thieves’ tools checks. Each failed check activates a freezing ray: +8 to hit, 13 (3d8)
cold damage.

2. Hall of Preserved Failures
  PRESERVED ITERATIONS
  Shapes stand within the walls of clear ice: wolves with too many lungs,
  humanoids plated in white chitin, and winged bears frozen mid-roar. Small brass
  plaques identify each body by number, climate, and survival time. The final
  alcove is empty. Its plaque reads: “ITERATION 47 - RELEASED FOR FIELD
  VALIDATION.”


The empty alcove belonged to the Polar Wyvern Krasis. Searching the chamber
finds research notes describing how cold suppresses unstable mutation but


strengthens successful grafts. A DC 16 Medicine check reveals several subjects
were still alive when frozen.

3. Thermal Inversion Room
This chamber extracts heat from living creatures and stores it in crystal batteries.
Crossing requires a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes
14 (4d6) cold damage and has its speed reduced by 10 feet for 10 minutes. The
machine can be disabled with a DC 16 Arcana check or destroyed. Three intact
thermal batteries are worth 150 gp each and can each be shattered to deal 10 fire
damage to all creatures in a 10-foot radius.

4. Captive Gallery
Tessa Vale and the missing glacier scouts are restrained in upright crystal pods.
Two Frostbound Wardens guard them, using Gladiator statistics with resistance to
cold damage. Freeing all captives before combat ends requires three actions and
DC 14 Arcana or thieves’ tools checks. Tessa knows Vaelgor can teleport between
three rune circles in the core chamber.

5. Relay Observatory
A crystal projection shows nodes throughout Vael Turog. Greymoor Hollow,
Stonewake Port, Emberfall, Lunareth, High Bastion, Marrowfen, Silverreach, and
Duskmire are marked as disrupted. Frostharbor flashes “FINAL CALIBRATION.”
Sunspire and Aetherfall remain bright.
Studying the projection for 10 minutes and succeeding on a DC 17 Arcana check
identifies the relay protocol. In the final battle, one character can use an action at a
control pedestal to make a DC 16 Arcana check. On a success, Vaelgor loses his
regeneration until the end of the next round.

6. The Glacial Lattice Core
  THE FINAL CALIBRATION
  The vault opens into a cavern large enough to hold a cathedral. A crystalline
  array hangs inside the glacier, each branch connected to a frozen creature, a
  metal pylon, or a vein of green light. At the center floats a tall figure wrapped in
  black robes and white armor. Frost burns glow green across his face. “Thermal
  extremes verified,” he says. “Structural integrity stable. Ascension threshold
  reached.”


Vaelgor’s Purpose
Vaelgor the White is a former Frostharbor mage who embraced Rabu Shin’s work
after losing his family to plague. He believes directed evolution can end suffering


and sees the glacial trials as necessary proof. He is willing to speak before combat
and offers the party a place in the ascension. He does not surrender the core.
A character who appeals to Vaelgor’s lost family and succeeds on a DC 19
Persuasion check causes him to hesitate. He begins combat without his Frost
Mantle temporary hit points. Presenting rescued victims or proof of House Varin’s
exploitation grants advantage on the check.

Boss Encounter: Frostbound Diabolist
Vaelgor fights alongside two Ice Mephits. He uses walls, teleportation circles, and
forced movement to separate the party. The three relay pylons around the
chamber have AC 16, 25 hit points, immunity to cold, poison, and psychic damage,
and vulnerability to thunder damage. Destroying a pylon weakens both Vaelgor
and the core.

Destroying the Core
When Vaelgor falls or all pylons are destroyed, the core becomes unstable. The
party has 5 rounds to shut it down before the glacier fractures. Each round, one
character adjacent to the core can attempt a DC 17 Arcana, Athletics, or thieves’
tools check. The party needs four successes before three failures. The Frost Cipher
grants one automatic success. A failed check deals 10 (3d6) cold or force damage to
the acting character.
If the party succeeds, the core shuts down in a controlled cascade and the captives
can escape safely. If the party fails, the core still collapses, but every character
must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) bludgeoning and cold
damage on a failure, or half as much on a success.

Rabu Shin’s Projection
  THE ARCHITECT SPEAKS
  The fractured core freezes in mid-collapse. Green light spreads across the cavern
  wall, forming the outline of a robed figure made from intersecting lines. Rabu
  Shin regards you with calm curiosity. “You misunderstand,” he says. “I am not
  destroying your world. I am removing its weaknesses.” The projection turns
  toward the dead core. “The body was merely a laboratory. I no longer require
  one.”


Coordinates appear beneath the projection: Sunspire, identified as the origin
laboratory, and Aetherfall, identified as the Ascension Nexus. The data confirms
Rabu Shin has encoded his consciousness into the lattice network. Destroying
individual laboratories can delay him, but only the Aetherfall engine can collapse
the distributed mind completely.

Escape from the Glacier
The vault begins to break apart after the projection ends. Run the escape as a
group challenge requiring three successes before two failures. Athletics,
Acrobatics, Survival, and Arcana are appropriate at DC 15. Rescued hunters,
harbor crews, the Pale Prism, or the Sunsteel Brazier can each convert one failure
into a success. On failure, each character takes 14 (4d6) damage and loses one
nonmagical item chosen by the player beneath the ice.


Chapter Conclusion
With the glacial core destroyed, the harbor ice stops moving in geometric patterns.
The lighthouse returns to a steady white beam, trapped vessels settle safely, and
the unnatural command over northern predators ends. Frostharbor begins a slow
recovery, though the scars within the glacier will remain for generations.
The party now possesses the Sunspire coordinates, the Ascension Protocol, and
proof that Rabu Shin exists as a distributed consciousness. Captain Rimeborn
provides a ship and winter supplies, while Sister Ylsa urges the characters to seek
the origin of the network before Aetherfall completes synchronization.

Milestone Advancement
Characters advance to Level 10 when they defeat Vaelgor, destroy or shut down
the Glacial Lattice Core, rescue at least some of the captives, and witness Rabu
Shin’s projection.

Failure and Escalation
If the party abandons Frostharbor, the glacial core completes transmission. Within
three days, the northern ice shelf fractures and trade collapses. Within seven days,
cold-adapted Krasis spread south. In the Aetherfall finale, Rabu Shin begins with
an additional legendary resistance and all cold-based Krasis gain 10 temporary hit
points.
If Vaelgor escapes, he appears in Sunspire as an additional guardian or warns
Rabu Shin of the party’s tactics. If the core is destroyed but the captives die,
Frostharbor survives yet regards the party with grief rather than celebration.

Transition to Sunspire
 SOUTHWARD TO THE ORIGIN
 For the first time in weeks, the lighthouse casts a clean white beam across the
 harbor. Beyond it, the northern sky clears, and the stars return. Among the data
 recovered from the glacier is a single route marked in Rabu Shin’s own hand: a
 line stretching south across Vael Turog to the buried towers of Sunspire. Beside it


  is one word. “Origin.”


The next chapter leads the characters to Sunspire, where they will enter Rabu
Shin’s first complete laboratory, recover the Ascension Engine blueprint, and learn
how to reach Aetherfall before the final synchronization begins.
