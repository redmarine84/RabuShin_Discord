Campaign Overview
Quests of Rabu Shin: Tales of the Krasis is a continent-spanning campaign of
investigation, dark fantasy, biomantic horror, siege warfare, and arcane
conspiracy. The adventure begins with local disturbances in Greymoor Hollow and
expands through twelve settlements as the characters uncover a linked network of
engineered mutation sites. Each region reveals a different stage of Rabu Shin’s
work: aquatic adaptation, volcanic resilience, ecosystem integration, military
coordination, amphibious fusion, political financing, mobile mutation, glacial
preservation, genome reconstruction, and finally ascension in the floating city of
Aetherfall.
The Krasis are not accidental monsters. They are deliberate combinations of living
creatures, elemental traits, arcane structures, and adaptive magic. Rabu Shin
believes natural evolution is too slow and too wasteful. Through crystalline lattice
nodes, hidden laboratories, corrupted relays, and willing patrons, he is
transforming Vael Turog into a proving ground for directed evolution.
Characters begin as travelers drawn to rumors of malformed livestock and dying
fields. By the finale, they must confront a distributed intelligence capable of
rewriting life across entire environments. Victory requires more than defeating
Rabu Shin. The heroes must decide whether the lattice should be destroyed,
contained, or reprogrammed—and what degree of change any person has the right
to impose upon another.

Running the Adventure
Adventure Structure
Each chapter is organized for quick use at the table. Settlement overviews establish
local tone; keyed locations provide NPCs, rumors, and clues; primary parts form
the chapter’s central path; optional parts deepen the settlement and provide
alternate advancement. Read-aloud passages appear in shaded boxes. Text outside
those boxes is intended for the Dungeon Master.

Character Advancement
The campaign uses hybrid milestone advancement. Characters advance when they
resolve the major threat of a settlement and recover the chapter’s critical evidence.
Optional quests provide treasure, allies, and additional context but are not
required for advancement unless the group bypasses a primary quest.

Difficulty and Scaling
 • For three characters, remove one minor creature from encounters containing
    four or more enemies.
 • For five or more characters, increase boss hit points by 20 percent or add one
    minor creature.
 • Do not increase every difficulty class. Let additional characters improve the
   party’s reliability.
 • When an encounter is described as cosmetic mutation only, use the listed
   standard stat block without mechanical changes.

The Lattice Clue Chain
The campaign’s investigations are cumulative. Early clues should not reveal the
whole conspiracy. Instead, repeat visual and magical motifs: geometric runes,
green or crimson pulses, metallic scents, crystalline residue, unnaturally
coordinated behavior, and fragments of Rabu Shin’s philosophy. The party should
gradually move from “something is wrong” to “someone is directing it” to “the
system can function without its creator.”
 DM Principle: Never hide the only path forward behind a single ability check. A
 successful check provides clarity, speed, or extra information. Failure introduces cost or
 uncertainty, not a dead end.


The Continent of Vael Turog
Vael Turog is a land shaped by extremes and endurance. Mountain ranges divide
its heart, ancient forests dominate the east, volcanic ridges smolder in the west,
and broad southern deserts conceal ruins beneath shifting dunes. Frozen seas
batter the northwest coast, while the floating city of Aetherfall hangs above the
central mountains as a monument to arcane ambition.
Twelve major settlements anchor civilization: Frostharbor in the northwest;
Stonewake Port on the western coast; Greymoor Hollow in the central plains;
Emberfall at a volcanic mountain; High Bastion atop the central range; Marrowfen
in marshlands east of Greymoor; Sunspire in a southwestern desert oasis;
Duskmire Crossing in the southeastern desert; Lunareth in an ancient eastern
forest; Aetherfall above High Bastion; Blackroot Enclave inside the mountains
south of Emberfall; and Silverreach east of High Bastion.

Adventure Hooks
Use one or more of the following hooks to bring the party together. The characters
should begin on the Lowroad within a mile of Greymoor Hollow.
Livestock Bounty. Mayor Harlowe has circulated word that Greymoor Hollow will
pay armed travelers to investigate malformed livestock and unexplained animal
attacks.
Spoiled Grain Shipment. A Stonewake merchant rejected Greymoor grain after
discovering that every kernel was hollow. The characters have been hired to
determine whether the shipment was sabotaged.
Missing Shepherd. Young Tomas vanished after claiming he saw glowing marks in
a pasture. A relative asks the characters to find him before nightfall.
Arcane Curiosity. A scholar in Lunareth has heard rumors of animals displaying
directed transmutation and asks the party to collect a sample.
Roadward Duty. The party is traveling through Greymoor when a wounded
farmer warns that wolves have become unnaturally bold along the Lowroad.
