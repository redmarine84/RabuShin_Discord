Chapter 11
        BLACKROOT ENCLAVE
      THE LAST UNALTERED STRONGHOLD
               Recommended Level 11 • Preparation for Level 12 Finale

 DM NOTE. Chapter Purpose. Blackroot Enclave is the campaign’s final refuge, alliance
 chapter, and strategic preparation point. The characters learn how to collapse Rabu Shin’s
 distributed consciousness without releasing a continent-wide mutation surge, earn
 temporary anti-lattice equipment, expose an infiltrator, and make the one-way journey to
 Aetherfall.


                             Blackroot Enclave settlement map


Blackroot Enclave
Blackroot Enclave lies inside a mountain south of Emberfall, reached through root-
choked caverns and ancient duergar tunnels. Its people are dwarves, druids,
rangers, artificers, and refugees who rejected uncontrolled arcane
experimentation generations ago. They do not oppose change; they oppose change
imposed without consent.
The settlement is built around the roots of an impossible subterranean tree whose
black bark grows through stone. Bioluminescent fungi illuminate iron bridges,


carved halls, living workshops, and deep containment vaults. Blackroot has studied
Rabu Shin’s lattice signatures since his exile and has prepared tools intended for
one purpose: surviving Aetherfall long enough to shut it down.

 ARRIVAL AT BLACKROOT
 The mountain passage narrows until roots as thick as ship masts fill the stone.
 Warm green light glows between them. A hidden gate of ironwood opens without
 a sound, revealing bridges suspended over a cavern vast enough to hold a city. At
 its center rises a black-barked tree, its crown lost in darkness. Hooded sentries
 level bows from the roots above. “If you carry lattice shards,” one calls, “declare
 them now.”


Entering the Enclave
The sentries insist that every lattice fragment be placed in a leadglass containment
coffer before the characters pass the Ironbark Gate. A character who refuses must
succeed on a DC 17 Persuasion check to earn a guarded audience, while concealed
fragments are detected by ward-stones unless hidden with nondetection or similar
magic.
Once the party explains the events at Sunspire, Archdruid Thalen orders the
fragments transferred to the Hollow Council Tree. The characters are treated as
honored but dangerous guests. Their actions during this chapter determine how
much support Blackroot commits to the final assault.

Keyed Locations in Blackroot Enclave
1. Ironbark Gate. The guarded entrance, lined with anti-transmutation runes and
lattice-detection stones.
2. Hollow Council Tree. A vast chamber grown inside the central root where the
enclave’s elders debate strategy.
3. Forge of Living Steel. Artisans combine ironwood, silver, radiant salts, and
memory crystal into temporary anti-lattice equipment.
4. Containment Vault. Captured constructs and infected specimens are held behind
layered wards. The party can train here before Aetherfall.
5. Rootbound Archive. Maps, survivor testimony, and resonance records chart the
entire lattice network.
6. Deepwell Sanctuary. A quiet refuge where healers examine the characters for
contamination and remove lingering mutation effects.
7. Gravity Chamber. A test hall built to simulate Aetherfall’s shifting orientation
and forced movement.
8. Gateway Hollow. A stone circle beneath the oldest roots. Once activated, it opens
a one-way route to Aetherfall’s outer ring.

Part 1: The Hollow Council
 COUNCIL OF ROOTS
 The council chamber is formed from the living interior of the great tree. Rings in
 the dark wood glow like constellations. Elders sit in carved alcoves while your
 recovered fragments float above a stone basin, each pulsing in time with the
 others. Archdruid Thalen studies the pattern. “You did not bring us keys,” he says.
 “You brought us pieces of a single mind.”


Thalen explains that Rabu Shin’s consciousness is distributed through the lattice,
but synchronization occurs in Aetherfall. Destroying the nexus will collapse the
network. If the engine is shattered while fully charged, however, every connected
node could discharge its stored biomantic energy into the surrounding region.
The council asks the party to help complete three preparations: calibrate a
containment sigil, test equipment in the Gravity Chamber, and investigate a
suspected infiltrator. Completing all three grants the full Blackroot Support
package.

Part 2: The Resonance Map
Archivist Kelm leads the characters into the Rootbound Archive, where hundreds
of suspended crystal needles trace pulses across a relief map of Vael Turog. Every
settlement visited during the campaign glows as a dim node. Aetherfall burns
above them like a second sun.

 THE NETWORK REVEALED
 Lines of pale light connect Greymoor Hollow, Stonewake Port, Emberfall,
 Lunareth, High Bastion, Marrowfen, Silverreach, Duskmire Crossing,
 Frostharbor, and Sunspire. Each pulse climbs toward a floating point above the
 map. When the lights meet, the air fills with a whisper spoken in thousands of
 overlapping voices: “Synchronization: ninety-seven percent.”


A successful DC 16 Arcana check identifies three vulnerable network layers:
regeneration, gravity control, and consciousness relay. The party can carry only
two specialized disruptors through the gateway, forcing a strategic choice.

Part 4: Trial of Shifting Gravity
 THE GRAVITY CHAMBER
 The circular chamber has no visible floor. Iron plates cover every surface, and
 chains hang in six directions. Brunna seals the door behind you. A bell rings once.
 Your stomach lurches as the wall becomes the ground and a training construct
 drops sideways toward you.


The trial lasts 4 rounds. At initiative count 20, gravity rotates to a new surface.
Every creature must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or fall 20 feet
toward the new floor, taking 2d6 bludgeoning damage and landing prone. A
creature secured by a chain or using a gravity anchor automatically succeeds.
The party faces 2 Reprogrammed Shield Guardians and 1 Helmed Horror. These
constructs deal nonlethal damage. The objective is to touch three control sigils on
different surfaces while avoiding being pinned by the guardians.
Success teaches the party how to exploit Aetherfall’s flux. Each character gains one
Gravity Adaptation token. During the final chapter, a token can be spent to reroll a
failed saving throw against a gravity effect.

Part 5: The Containment Vault
Sister Olyra asks the party to witness Blackroot’s captured specimens before
deciding the fate of Rabu Shin’s work. The vault contains lesser constructs, failed
Krasis, and one Flesh-Construct Angel restrained inside a ring of roots and silver.

 THE CAPTURED ANGEL


 The figure kneels inside the ward, wings folded like blades. Its body is porcelain-
 white flesh over golden framework. When you enter, every eye along its wings
 opens. “You are late,” it says in Rabu Shin’s voice. “The city has already begun
 dreaming of you.”


The angel does not immediately attack. It answers three questions truthfully
before attempting to overload the ward. Useful revelations include:
• Rabu Shin monitors the party through residual lattice traces collected during
   earlier chapters.
• Aetherfall’s three outer nodes protect the Prime Krasis with regeneration,
  legendary action capacity, and resistance to containment.
• The Gateway of Realities is not a portal to another world; it is a device for
   projecting adaptation instructions across this one.
• Rabu Shin believes the party’s survival validates his theory and intends to offer
   them ascension.
After the third answer, the angel triggers a containment breach. The party must
either defeat it or restore the wards with 3 successful DC 16 Arcana or Religion
checks before 2 failures. A character can make a check as an action. Restoring the
wards captures the angel intact and grants Kelm valuable data; destroying it
prevents further danger.

Warden Erynd reports that Scout Varik Thorn returned from a surveillance
mission with no visible wounds, yet his memories contain impossible gaps. Varik is
secretly a remote Krasis agent carrying a seed-code designed to infect Blackroot’s
central tree when the gateway opens.

 VARIK’S WARNING
 Varik grips the edge of the table until his knuckles split. Green light leaks through
 the wounds. “It hears every word,” he gasps. “Do not open the gate while I am
 here.” His shadow stretches toward the central tree though the lanterns burn
 behind him. “Please. I can feel it learning the roots.”


Chapter Boss: Lattice Infiltrator
If the extraction fails or the party ignores Varik, he attacks in the Gateway Hollow
during activation. His objective is not to defeat the heroes but to reach the root-
heart and inject the seed-code. Place the root-heart 60 feet from his starting
position.
• At the end of each of his turns, the infiltrator moves up to 15 feet toward the root-
  heart without provoking opportunity attacks.
• A character adjacent to a control root can use an action and succeed on a DC 15
  Nature or Arcana check to raise a barrier, reducing the infiltrator’s movement
  by 15 feet for one round.
• If the infiltrator reaches the root-heart, Blackroot’s wards fail and all characters
   begin the Aetherfall chapter with one fewer Blackroot enhancement charge.
• Radiant damage suppresses the infiltrator’s regeneration until the end of its next
   turn.

Part 7: Final Preparations
After the infiltrator is stopped, Blackroot completes the containment sigil. The
party receives a full rest, replacement ammunition, spell components worth up to
500 gp per spellcaster, and one superior healing potion each. Sister Olyra can
remove one curse, disease, or lingering mutation from every character.
The council also offers the following shared equipment:
• Three Greater Potions of Healing.
• Two Potions of Flying, intended for broken Aetherfall bridges.
• One Scroll of Mass Cure Wounds.
• One Blackroot Containment Seal, required to stabilize or reprogram the
  Ascension Engine.
• A resonance compass that points toward the nearest active Aetherfall node.


Optional Blackroot Quests
The Root That Remembers
A dying root contains memories of Rabu Shin visiting Blackroot before his exile.
Entering the memory requires a DC 15 group Wisdom saving throw. The party
witnesses Thalen refusing to help weaponize adaptive magic and learns the phrase
“Choice is the boundary of life.” Speaking this phrase during the final
confrontation grants advantage on one social check against Rabu Shin.

The Broken Wardstone
A wardstone in the lower caverns has been shattered by a tunneling Krasis.
Repairing it requires defeating 2 Bulette-like Root Borers and completing 3 DC 15
Arcana or Mason’s Tools checks. Success grants Blackroot enough power to send
one healing pulse through the gateway during Aetherfall, restoring 4d8 + 4 hit
points to the party once.

Voices Below the Forge
Workers hear whispers beneath the Forge of Living Steel. The source is a buried
relay splinter attempting to mimic Brunna’s voice. Destroying it reveals an
Aetherfall maintenance diagram and gives the party advantage on checks to
disable the first tower node.

The Unaltered Seed
Thalen asks the party to recover a seed from the deepest root chamber before the
gateway drains the area’s magic. The chamber is guarded by a Stone Golem
overgrown with roots. Planting the seed after the campaign can restore one region
damaged by lattice collapse.

A Question of Mercy
A stable Krasis captive retains its original personality and asks to be released. The
council is divided. Freeing, containing, or euthanizing the captive has no
mechanical answer, but the choice should influence the party’s later decision
about destroying, stabilizing, or reprogramming the engine.

The One-Way Gateway
 THE SKY OPENS
 The gateway circle ignites in silver-green light. Roots pull away from the stone as
 gravity bends toward the center. Through the widening aperture, clouds move
 below a city of crystal, bone, and impossible bridges. Archdruid Thalen presses the
 containment seal into your hands. “When you return,” he says, “the world will be
 different.” The floor vanishes. You fall upward.


Once activated, the connection remains open for 1 minute and cannot be reopened
from Blackroot. The party may delay before entering, but every hour of delay


advances Aetherfall’s synchronization by 1 percent. At 100 percent, the final
chapter begins with an immediate global mutation pulse.

Chapter Resolution
Blackroot Enclave is not saved by defeating a single enemy. It is preserved by
preventing the seed-code, completing the containment plan, and leaving with a
clear understanding of the stakes. The party does not automatically gain a level
here; characters should reach Level 12 immediately before entering Aetherfall,
either through milestone advancement now or through the campaign’s preferred
progression method.
The characters now know that defeating Rabu Shin is only the first half of the
finale. They must also decide what to do with the Ascension Engine before its
stored energy escapes.

Rewards and Advancement
• Milestone. Advance the characters to Level 12 when they enter the Gateway
  Hollow for the final assault.
• One Blackroot enhancement per character.
• Two network disruptors chosen by the party.
• Blackroot Containment Seal.
• Shared healing supplies and resonance compass.
• Gravity Adaptation token for each character who completes the training trial.
