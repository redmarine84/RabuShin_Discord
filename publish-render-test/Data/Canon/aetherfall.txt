Final Chapter: Aetherfall - The Ascension of
Krasis
Recommended Level: 12
Expected Party Strength: Fully rested and equipped with Blackroot enhancements
Campaign Finale: No further advancement is required.
 AETHERFALL
 The gateway opens above the clouds. Gravity catches the characters and pulls them upward
 toward a city suspended against the stars. Towers bend inward around a radiant core of
 crystal and bone. Bridges curve across open sky, waterfalls stream into nothing, and
 geometric veins of silver-green light connect every structure. At the city’s center, a vast ring
 turns around an opening filled with impossible horizons. A calm voice speaks through every
 stone: “You have arrived precisely on schedule.”


Chapter Overview
Aetherfall is both a city and a continent-spanning arcane machine. Rabu Shin has encoded
his consciousness into its lattice and intends to broadcast directed evolution through every
node the characters encountered during the campaign. The final chapter is structured as a
sequence of objectives rather than a single dungeon corridor. The party must disable the
city’s defenses, sever the consciousness relay, confront the Prime Krasis, and decide the fate
of the Ascension Engine.

Phase I: The Fractured Causeway
 FALLING UPWARD
 Cold wind tears at you as the gateway closes below. The world curves beneath your feet,
 distant and blue. Ahead, shattered roads float in layers around Aetherfall’s outer ring.
 Statues of winged figures turn their blank faces toward you. One after another, their eyes
 ignite with green light.


The party appears on three linked platforms 100 feet from the Shattered Ring. If Blackroot’s
gateway calibration failed, divide the characters between two adjacent platforms. Reuniting
requires crossing a 20-foot gap by jumping, flying, rope, or a successful DC 14 group Athletics
or Acrobatics check.

Outer-Ring Defenders
The causeway is guarded by 1 Flesh-Construct Angel and 2 Shield Guardians. The angel
begins airborne and attempts to force characters from the road. The guardians hold the
narrow crossing and use their reactions to protect the angel.
A character carrying the Resonance Compass can use a bonus action to identify the first node
beneath a broken obelisk. The node has AC 17, 50 hit points, immunity to poison and psychic
damage, and resistance to all damage until a character uses an action to succeed on a DC 16
Arcana, Investigation, or Thieves’ Tools check to expose its core.

Node One: Regeneration Matrix
Disabling this node removes Rabu Shin’s Regeneration trait during Phase 1 of the final battle.
If the party bypasses it, Rabu Shin regains 15 hit points at the start of each turn unless he took
radiant damage since his previous turn.
 RABU SHIN OBSERVES
 “You have learned to target systems rather than symptoms. Efficient. Blackroot has
 improved you.” The angel’s broken face turns toward the speaker’s voice, though the words
 come from every direction. “Continue.”


Phase II: The Angelic Perimeter
Beyond the Shattered Ring, the party can approach either the Twin Halo Towers or Sorcerer’s
Rise. Both routes contain one defensive node. The party may complete them in either order,
split up, or skip one to conserve resources.


Route A: Twin Halo Towers
 THE HALO TOWERS
 Two needle-thin towers orbit one another without touching. A bridge of transparent light
 stretches between them. Above the bridge hangs a circular halo filled with hundreds of
 preserved faces, each whispering a different possible future.


The towers are guarded by 2 Flesh-Construct Angels. At initiative count 20, the halo selects
one creature. That creature must make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or become restrained
by visions until the end of its next turn. A creature can use an action to shatter a face within
the halo, granting the target an immediate repeat of the save.

Node Two: Legendary Conduit
The node is suspended inside the halo. Reaching it requires flight, a DC 16 Athletics check
while climbing the rotating tower, or activation of a Gravity Anchor Array. Destroying or
disrupting it reduces Rabu Shin’s available legendary actions from 3 to 2. If both the
Legendary Conduit and Consciousness Relay are disabled, he has only 1 legendary action per
round in the final phase.

Route B: Sorcerer’s Rise
 SORCERER’S RISE
 A staircase spirals upward through empty air, each step appearing only after weight touches
 the one below. At the summit, six robed figures stand around a crystal pillar. Their faces are
 smooth mirrors. In each reflection, you see yourself wearing a different body.


The six figures are Lattice Echoes. Use the Mage stat block with 45 hit points, AC 15, and no
5th-level spells. They coordinate counterspells and use mirror image, misty step, lightning
bolt, and hold person. When an echo falls, its mirror-face cracks and releases the memory of
a person absorbed by the lattice.

Node Three: Resistance Matrix
The crystal pillar adapts to damage. At the end of each round, it gains resistance to the
damage type that dealt the most damage to it that round. It has AC 16 and 60 hit points. A Sigil
Disruptor removes all resistances until the end of the next round. Disabling the node
prevents Rabu Shin from gaining resistance through Mutation Surge result 3 and removes
one Legendary Resistance.

Optional Objective: The Mystic Stronghold
The stronghold contains prisoners whose minds were copied during Rabu Shin’s
experiments. Releasing them requires three successful DC 16 Arcana, Religion, or Medicine
checks before two failures. Success grants the party the Memory Chorus: once during the
final battle, one character can reroll a failed Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma saving throw.
Failure awakens an Aetherfall Abomination.
 THE PRESERVED MINDS
 Crystal cylinders line the chamber. Within each, light twists into the outline of a person: a
 miner from Emberfall, a sailor from Stonewake, a soldier from High Bastion, a child from
 Silverreach. Their voices overlap. “He said we would survive. He never said we would remain
 ourselves.”

All routes converge on a district of rotating streets between the outer city and Mystic Spiral.
The labyrinth is a moving skill challenge: 6 successes before 3 failures. Each character must

Phase IV: The Mystic Spiral
 THE CONSCIOUSNESS RELAY
 The tower’s interior is wider than the city outside. Rings of roots, crystal, eyes, and polished
 metal rotate around a vertical void. A colossal shape hangs at the center: a beholder’s many-
 eyed crown fused into the trunk of an ancient tree. Its roots disappear into thousands of
 glowing memories. Every eye opens at once.


The Archbloom Orbweaver guards the consciousness relay. The battle occurs on four circular
platforms rotating around the central void. At initiative count 20, each platform rotates 90
degrees and moves 10 feet vertically. A creature standing within 5 feet of an edge must
succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or fall to the next platform, taking 2d6
bludgeoning damage and landing prone.

Relay Roots
Four relay roots connect the Orbweaver to the tower. Each root has AC 16, 30 hit points,
immunity to psychic and poison damage, and vulnerability to radiant damage. A root
destroyed during the battle reduces the Orbweaver’s regeneration by 5. Destroying all four
shuts down its Mythic Reawakening and severs Rabu Shin’s distributed echo defense.

Victory at the Spiral
When the Orbweaver falls, the characters can sever the consciousness relay with the
Consciousness Severance Seal or with 3 successful DC 17 Arcana, Religion, or Nature checks.
On a failure, the acting character takes 4d8 psychic damage. Severing the relay removes Rabu
Shin’s Lattice Echo phase. If it remains active, he divides into three echoes when reduced to
half hit points.
 RABU SHIN’S ADDRESS


 The tower goes silent. A figure forms from the falling fragments: tall, calm, and threaded
 with geometric light. “You have exceeded projected survivability parameters. Most subjects
 fracture long before this stage. You adapt. Good.” He steps across the air as though gravity
 were optional. “You have mistaken my work for corruption. It is correction.”


Rabu Shin’s Offer
Rabu Shin does not attack immediately. He offers the characters ascension, promising
freedom from aging, disease, and death. Allow each player to answer. A character who
engages him in debate can make one DC 18 Persuasion, Insight, Arcana, Nature, or Religion
check. Each unique success reveals one truth and grants one Conviction token. The party can
spend a Conviction token to add 1d6 to any saving throw during the final battle.

Phase V: The Gateway of Realities
 THE FINAL ARENA
 The Gateway of Realities turns above an endless void. Through it flicker forests, oceans,
 deserts, glaciers, and cities - every region touched by the lattice. Rabu Shin stands on a
 circular platform of crystal and living bone. Behind him, a thousand threads descend toward
 Vael Turog. “Aetherfall is not a throne,” he says. “It is proof.”


The final arena is a 100-foot-diameter circular platform surrounded by four pylons. Each
pylon has AC 18, 45 hit points, immunity to poison and psychic damage, and resistance to
nonmagical attacks. Destroying a pylon suppresses one battlefield effect and contributes to
safely resolving the Ascension Engine.

Final Battle: Rabu Shin, the Prime Krasis
Phase 1 - Architect of Flesh
Rabu Shin begins with calculated battlefield control. He uses wall of force, reverse gravity,
disintegrate, and his Warp Lash to separate characters and test their adaptations. He speaks
throughout the battle and identifies successful tactics as though recording data.

Phase 2 - Distributed Consciousness
If the Mystic Spiral relay remains active, when Rabu Shin is first reduced to 125 hit points his
body fractures into three Lattice Echoes. Each echo uses the Mage stat block with 60 hit
points, AC 17, resistance to nonmagical damage, and one of the following themes: Force,
Flesh, or Gravity. Rabu Shin cannot be damaged until all echoes are destroyed. If the relay
was severed, skip this phase and Rabu Shin instead loses one Legendary Resistance.

Phase 3 - Prime Krasis
When Rabu Shin is reduced to 100 hit points after the echo phase, or immediately upon
reaching 100 hit points if the phase was skipped, he fuses with the Ascension Engine. His size
becomes Large, his reach increases by 5 feet, and he gains the Prime Form features in his stat
block. The platform fractures into four sections. At initiative count 10 each round, one
unoccupied 10-foot square falls into the void.
 PRIME KRASIS
 Rabu Shin’s body opens into wings of crystalline bone and luminous tendrils. His voice now
 comes from the city, the gateway, and inside your own thoughts. “You cling to fragility. You
 romanticize decay. I sought to free you from hunger, plague, and death.” The platform splits
 beneath him. “Adapt.”


Defeating Rabu Shin
At 0 hit points, Rabu Shin does not die immediately. His body collapses into a geometric
outline while the Ascension Engine begins an uncontrolled broadcast. Initiative continues.
The party has 3 rounds to choose and complete one resolution. A character may use an action
to attempt a required check, operate a pylon, protect an ally, or spend a Blackroot device for
an automatic success when appropriate.

Final Choice: Destroy the Engine
Destroying the engine requires dealing 100 damage to its exposed core within 3 rounds. The
core has AC 15, immunity to poison and psychic damage, and vulnerability to force and
radiant damage. Each destroyed pylon reduces the required damage by 15. Success
permanently collapses the lattice network, but Aetherfall begins to fall.
Escape requires 4 group successes before 2 failures using Athletics, Acrobatics, Arcana,
Perception, or Blackroot equipment. On success, the Blackroot Containment Seal opens a
return corridor at the last moment. On failure, each character takes 10d6 force damage
before being thrown into the gateway; survivors land in a random settlement.

Final Choice: Stabilize and Shut Down
Stabilization requires 5 successes before 3 failures using Arcana, Religion, Nature, Smith’s
Tools, or the Resonance Phrase recovered in Sunspire. The base DC is 18, reduced by 1 for
each disabled outer node or destroyed pylon. On success, Aetherfall slowly descends to a
stable altitude and the lattice becomes inert. On failure, resolve the Mutation Surge table
below, then allow one final attempt at DC 20.


Final Choice: Reprogram the Lattice
Reprogramming requires the Blackroot Containment Seal, the Ascension Engine Blueprint,
and 5 successes before 3 failures at DC 19. Arcana, Nature, Religion, Medicine, and Persuasion
may be used, but each skill can contribute only once. The party must define a governing
principle such as voluntary adaptation, healing only, ecological restoration, or complete
individual consent. Success preserves the network under that limitation. Failure allows the
surviving fragment of Rabu Shin to influence the new directive.

Rabu Shin’s Final Words
 AT ZERO HIT POINTS
 “So this is your answer.” He does not scream. Light fractures through him as the engine tears
 open. “Understand this: evolution does not require me. You adapted to defeat me. You
 changed.” His gaze passes across each of you. “You are my proof.”


If the engine is destroyed, his final whisper is: “Iteration continues.”
If the engine is stabilized, his final whisper is: “You chose control. Let us see how long you
maintain it.”
If the engine is reprogrammed, his final whisper is: “Refinement... collaborative.”


Campaign Resolution
If Aetherfall Is Destroyed
The lattice goes silent across Vael Turog. Mutated creatures remain changed, but no new
directed transformations occur. Aetherfall collapses in a radiant implosion visible from every
settlement. Rabu Shin becomes a warning told by arcanists, soldiers, farmers, and survivors.

If Aetherfall Is Stabilized
Aetherfall survives as an inert floating city. Lunareth and Blackroot establish a joint order to
guard its archives. The party must decide who controls access, whether victims may recover
stored memories, and whether any portion of the research should be preserved.


If the Lattice Is Reprogrammed
Controlled adaptation becomes possible, but every use becomes a moral and political
decision. Silverreach seeks commercial access, High Bastion requests military applications,
Marrowfen asks for ecological restoration, and Frostharbor hopes to prevent future famine.
The characters become guardians of a power that can heal the world or quietly repeat Rabu
Shin’s mistake.

Final Boxed Text
 THE LATTICE IS SILENT
 The sky clears. Across Vael Turog, green light fades from rivers, roots, crystal, ice, and stone.
 For a moment the world holds its breath. Then wind moves through the trees. Waves strike
 the shore. Somewhere far below, a sheep gives an ordinary, irritated bleat. For the first time
 since Greymoor Hollow, the lattice is silent.
