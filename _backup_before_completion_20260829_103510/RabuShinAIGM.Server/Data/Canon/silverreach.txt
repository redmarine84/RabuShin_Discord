CHAPTER 7
                      SILVERREACH
              THE CONSPIRACY OF GOLD
   Recommended Levels 8-9 • Starting Level 8 • Hybrid Milestone Advancement
Chapter Purpose. Silverreach changes the campaign from wilderness
investigation to political exposure. The characters follow House Varin’s financial
trail through a wealthy trade capital, infiltrate a noble gala, discover a relay office
beneath the Varin estate, and confront a Krasis Noble who can blink through
space. This chapter reveals that influential citizens knowingly financed Rabu Shin’s
work because they expect to profit from controlled evolution. Characters should
reach 9th level after exposing the conspiracy and shutting down its funding
network.


                              Silverreach settlement map


Silverreach
Silverreach is Vael Turog’s richest inland city, a place of marble avenues, river
trade, noble estates, and guild influence. Public life is polished and ceremonial.
Behind closed doors, contracts matter more than laws, and respectable patrons can
purchase distance from the consequences of their decisions.
                                ARRIVAL IN SILVERREACH
   Sunlight flashes from golden domes and polished white stone. Carriages crowd
   the broad avenue leading toward the King’s Hall, while merchants call prices
   beneath silk awnings. The air smells of perfume, river water, and hot coin. As
   you pass, a noblewoman lowers her voice. A guard closes a ledger. Somewhere
   behind the splendor, someone already knows you have arrived.


Starting the Chapter
 • The Varin Manifest. Records recovered in Marrowfen show House Varin
    purchased pumps, preservatives, guards, and living specimens through
    charitable accounts.
 • High Bastion’s Accusation. Captain Stonehand sends the party as witnesses to
   prove Silverreach money funded the siege prototypes.
 • A Missing Clerk. A frightened accountant named Lio Venn disappeared after
   copying ledgers tied to Emberfall, Marrowfen, and Frostharbor.
 • The Gala Invitation. The Celestial Temple asks the party to attend House Varin’s
    public benefit and quietly investigate its claims of medical philanthropy.
 Chapter Flow: The party establishes allies, follows financial and human evidence, and
 gains access to the Sapphire Gala. A social infiltration reveals the hidden archive and the
 estate vault below it. The final battle occurs among unstable wards and relay machinery,
 followed by a choice to expose House Varin publicly or dismantle its network in secret.


Keyed Locations in Silverreach
1. The Golden Cup Tavern. A fashionable inn where merchants, junior nobles,
and visiting officers exchange rumors. Proprietor Maera Goldcup remembers faces
and debts.
2. The King’s Hall. Administrative center of Silverreach. Magistrates hesitate to
challenge House Varin without undeniable evidence.
3. The Celestial Temple. A powerful temple and refuge for victims of illegal
experimentation. High Priestess Auriel opposes House Varin quietly.
4. Brightwater Smithy. A respected forge whose owner found unusual bone-
hardening compounds in Varin shipments.
5. The Alchemist’s Emporium. Supplier of rare reagents. Its books reveal
purchases routed through shell charities.


6. Goldenroot Alchemy. A tea room and discreet apothecary where informants
meet beneath the noise of respectable business.
7. Lavina’s Ceramics. A workshop that unknowingly produced sealed ceramic
canisters for transporting tissue samples.
8. House Varin Estate. A walled noble compound containing the Sapphire Gala
hall, private archive, guard barracks, and underground relay vault.
9. Lower Dock Ward. Warehouse district where paid volunteers and kidnapped
laborers were processed before shipment.

Part 1: A City of Masks
The party needs three kinds of proof: financial records, testimony from a victim or
employee, and physical evidence of an active lattice operation. Any two provide
access to House Varin’s gala through allies; all three grant advantage on the
chapter’s final public accusation.

The Golden Cup Cipher
Maera Goldcup gives the party a brass key left by Lio Venn. It opens a private
bottle cabinet containing a page of payment codes. Investigation DC 14 links the
codes to Marrowfen pumps, High Bastion mercenaries, and Frostharbor shipping.
A watcher attempts to steal the page after the party leaves.

The Lower Dock Volunteers
Two injured dockworkers display reinforced bone grafts and unusual resistance to
pain. Medicine DC 15 confirms deliberate alteration. One admits they were paid by
Varin agents but says several others never returned. Following the recruiting route
leads to Warehouse 17.

The Alchemical Paper Trail
The Alchemist’s Emporium recorded large purchases of preservatives,
transmutation salts, and memory-dulling draughts. Persuasion DC 15 or proof from
Marrowfen convinces the owner to release copies. Failure alerts House Varin that
the party is tracing suppliers.

The Celestial Temple Patients
Auriel shelters three former test subjects. A gentle interview and DC 14 Persuasion
or Medicine check earns testimony. Pressuring them causes Auriel to withdraw
support and makes the gala infiltration more difficult.
                              THE COST OF IMPROVEMENT
   Behind a silk curtain in the temple infirmary, a young courier raises an arm
   wrapped in linen. Beneath the bandages, pale ridges move under the skin like
   roots testing soil. “They said I would never break again,” he whispers. “They
   did not tell me I would still feel it growing.”


Part 2: Warehouse 17
                                   THE HIDDEN CLINIC
   The warehouse stands apart from its neighbors, freshly painted and without a
   sign. No dockhands work its doors. Through a narrow window you see white
   tables, restraint straps, and ceramic jars stamped with the Varin crest. A bell


   rings once inside. Footsteps stop.


Warehouse 17 is guarded by four House Veterans and one Varin Biomancer. The
guards surrender if shown proof that the city watch is coming; the biomancer
destroys records and flees toward the estate if possible.

Key Areas
1. Receiving Floor
Crates contain surgical instruments, tissue preservatives, and restraint frames. A
shipping board lists Frostharbor as the next destination.

2. Recovery Cots
Three conscious volunteers and two kidnapped laborers lie sedated. Freeing them
provides witnesses and reduces House Varin’s public support.

3. Procedure Room
A half-completed grafting apparatus holds a dead lattice construct. Arcana DC 16
identifies a remote signal from beneath the Varin estate.

4. Records Office
A trapped cabinet releases soporific gas (DC 15 Constitution save or unconscious
for 1 minute). Inside are payroll books and a gala access token.

5. River Exit
A concealed boat tunnel allows captured evidence or rescued victims to be moved
discreetly to the Celestial Temple.
 Nonlethal Stakes: Most guards are ordinary employees told they are protecting medical
 research. Their surrender and testimony are more valuable than their deaths. Make the
 moral difference between willing profiteers, frightened workers, and altered victims
 visible.


Optional Silverreach Quests
Counterfeit Reagents
A rival merchant is selling fake anti-mutation tonics. Exposing the fraud requires
testing three samples and confronting hired thugs. Reward: 300 gp and two potions
of greater healing.

Guild Sabotage
A warehouse fire threatens evidence connecting House Varin to High Bastion.
Saving the records is a 4-success skill challenge. Success gives Inspector Dain legal
authority to raid the estate after the gala.

The Vanished Street Children
Six children are hidden in a sealed cellar awaiting transport. Rescuing them earns
Auriel’s full support and causes one Varin guard unit to abandon the estate.

The Blackroot Liaison
A duergar emissary from Blackroot Enclave offers a sigil disruptor in exchange for
a copy of the Varin relay coordinates. Accepting creates an early alliance and
grants one use of suppressing regeneration for 1 round.

Mutation Panic
A volunteer suffers an uncontrolled transformation in the Golden Forum. Calming
rather than killing the victim earns public trust and proves the procedures are
unstable.


Part 3: The Sapphire Gala
                                  THE GALA BEGINS
   Crystal chandeliers scatter blue light across a hall of masked nobles.
   Musicians play from a balcony while servants carry silver trays beneath
   banners reading PROGRESS THROUGH COMPASSION. At the far end, Lady
   Miravel Varin raises a glass beside a display of gleaming surgical instruments.
   “Tonight,” she announces, “Silverreach funds a future without frailty.”

should contribute, and repeated use of the same skill increases the DC by 2. Base
DC is 15.

Roleplaying House Varin
Lady Miravel argues that every advancement has demanded sacrifice and that
history remembers results, not discomfort. Lord Cassian focuses on cured soldiers,
restored workers, and children who might survive disease. Neither considers
themselves evil. Miravel values power; Cassian values outcomes and can be turned
if shown living victims and falsified safety reports.
                                A PATRON’S DEFENSE
   Lady Miravel’s smile does not falter when you mention Marrowfen. “You saw a
   laboratory,” she says. “I saw a generation of knowledge purchased in months


   rather than centuries. History is always expensive. The only honest question is
   who pays.”


Part 4: Beneath House Varin
The private archive conceals a lift descending to an underground relay office.
Unless noted otherwise, the vault is lit by blue-white glyphs and guarded by wards
keyed to Varin signets.

1. Private Archive
Ledgers are stored in rotating brass cabinets. The complete financial record links
House Varin to Emberfall, Marrowfen, High Bastion, and Frostharbor. Removing
the ledgers without a signet triggers an alarm.

2. Hall of Benefactors
Portraits of donors animate as magical sentries. Treat them as two Helmed Horrors
reskinned as gilded portrait guardians.

3. Compliance Chamber
A crystal device extracts memories from altered subjects. Destroying it frees three
psychic echoes that reveal the phrase “Glacial phase complete.”

4. Contract Vault
Signed agreements promise House Varin exclusive access to post-ascension
treatments. One contract bears no signature, only the letters R.S.

5. Ward Control
Four pillars power the final chamber’s hazards. Each can be disabled with DC 16
Arcana or thieves’ tools. Every disabled pillar removes one possible lair effect.

6. Financial Relay
A circular chamber projects trade routes as lines of green light. At its center stands
Veyra Sorn, her noble robes fused to a shifting lattice carapace.
 Resting in the Estate: A short rest is possible after the Compliance Chamber if the alarm
 has not sounded. A long rest allows House Varin to evacuate key witnesses and sends the
 Frostharbor warning ahead, increasing the next chapter’s alert level.


Part 5: The Krasis Noble
                                        VEYRA SORN
     Green lines race across the floor and climb the woman standing beside the
     relay. Her outline splits into three overlapping images. One smiles. One draws
     a dagger. One is already behind you. “Gold,” she says, “is the simplest mutation
     catalyst. It reshapes priorities before it ever reshapes flesh.”


Battlefield Features
• Ward Pillars. Up to four active pillars create effects at initiative count 20. Each
  has AC 16, 20 hit points, and immunity to poison and psychic damage.
• Relay Dais. A creature standing on the dais can use an action and DC 17 Arcana
  check to interrupt Veyra’s Aberrant Blink until the end of her next turn.
• Ledger Shelves. Shelves provide half cover. A creature can topple one with a DC
  15 Strength check, creating difficult terrain in a 10-foot line.
• Glass Memory Orbs. Breaking an orb releases a psychic scream; creatures within
  10 feet make a DC 15 Wisdom save or take 2d8 psychic damage.

Veyra’s Tactics
Veyra begins displaced, using spells and Warp Lash to separate the party. She
targets anyone carrying the Marrowfen Relay Crystal. When reduced below half
hit points, she uses Controlled Mutation to gain resistance to the most recent
damage type that harmed her. She offers surrender only if the party agrees to
preserve the financial relay.


The Evidence Decision
After Veyra falls, the relay begins erasing records. The party has 3 rounds to
choose priorities. Each round, one character can secure one category with a
successful DC 16 Arcana, Investigation, or thieves’ tools check: financial ledgers,
victim records, Frostharbor coordinates, or donor identities. A failure destroys that
category unless another character spends the next round recovering it.


Aftermath
With the relay disabled, House Varin’s protection fractures. The party must decide
whether to expose everything publicly or work through the King’s Hall and
Celestial Temple to dismantle the network quietly.

                              THE CONSPIRACY EXPOSED
   By sunrise, the golden banners over House Varin hang torn and wet with river
   mist. Bells ring from the Celestial Temple. In the King’s Hall, sealed ledgers
   strike a marble table one after another. On the final page, beside a shipment of
   glacial stabilizers, a single message waits: “Ascension threshold approaching.
   Send witnesses north.”


Rewards
 • Silverreach authorities award 1,200 gp if the party provides sufficient evidence.
 • The Celestial Temple grants three potions of greater healing and one scroll of
    greater restoration.
 • The party receives a Varin Signet Key, which grants advantage on one check to
    bypass a lattice-funded security system.


 • Veyra carries an Arcane Displacement Brooch. Once per long rest, its wearer can
   teleport 15 feet as a bonus action after being targeted by an attack.
 • Recovered accounts reveal supply caches in Frostharbor, allowing the party to
   begin the next chapter with cold-weather gear and two antitoxin doses.

Milestone Advancement
Characters advance to 9th level after shutting down the financial relay, exposing
House Varin’s connection to the Krasis network, and securing the Frostharbor
coordinates.

If House Varin Escapes
 • Funding continues through hidden accounts, strengthening later Krasis
    encounters.
 • Silverreach begins offering voluntary augmentation to nobles and soldiers.
 • Veyra or another Varin agent appears in Frostharbor with advance warning.
 • The Aetherfall finale begins with one additional financial relay that can restore a
    destroyed tower node once.

Transition to Frostharbor
                             THE NORTHERN SHIPMENT
   The secured relay projects a frozen coastline. Beneath the image, lines of
   precise script update in real time: THERMAL EXTREMES VERIFIED. GLACIAL
   CALIBRATION AT 93%. A ship leaves Silverreach before dawn carrying sealed
   crystal components north. Its destination is Frostharbor.
