Chapter 8
         DUSKMIRE CROSSING
              CARAVANS OF THE CHANGED
Recommended Level 9 • Bridge Chapter • No Automatic Level Advancement

 DM NOTE. Chapter Purpose. Duskmire Crossing reveals that Rabu Shin’s experiments are
 no longer limited to fixed laboratories. The Krasis network now uses trade caravans,
 fatigue, fear, and environmental stress to trigger dormant mutations in ordinary
 travelers. This chapter links House Varin’s funding network to the glacial project in
 Frostharbor while giving the party a mobile, investigation-driven threat.


                            Duskmire Crossing settlement map


Duskmire Crossing
Duskmire Crossing stands where the eastern trade road divides between
Silverreach, Sunspire, Lunareth, and the southern caravan routes. The settlement
grew around a stone bridge, a crowded bazaar, wagon yards, and inns built to
shelter travelers from dust storms and bandit raids. Its people are accustomed to
strangers, shortages, and danger. They are not accustomed to caravans arriving
with passengers who no longer remember what they were.


In recent weeks, three merchant trains have reached the Crossing late, damaged,
and nearly silent. Survivors display hardened skin, trembling limbs, distorted
shadows, and sudden bursts of violence. Every affected caravan carried sealed
medical supplies purchased through House Varin accounts. The supplies are not
medicine. They are activation agents designed to awaken dormant lattice grafts
under stress.

  ARRIVAL AT THE CROSSING
  Heat shimmers above the road as the trade settlement comes into view. Canvas
  awnings crowd both sides of a broad river crossing, and wagon wheels grind
  over stone worn smooth by generations of travel. Merchants call prices through
  the dust. Stablehands drag nervous animals toward water. Then a bell rings from
  the eastern road. A caravan has arrived. No driver guides the lead wagon. Its
  horses walk in perfect step, their reins dragging through the sand.


Starting the Chapter
The chapter begins immediately after the party leaves Silverreach or while they
are following the northern shipping records recovered from House Varin. The
secured documents identify a caravan code—DUSK-17—carrying preserved tissue,
alchemical triggers, and a courier bound for Frostharbor. The shipment should
have passed through Duskmire Crossing six days ago. It never arrived at its next
checkpoint.
• The Varin Ledger. Inspector Dain asks the party to intercept DUSK-17 and recover
   its courier case before House Varin’s remaining agents can destroy it.
• A Missing Caravan. A merchant family offers 500 gp for proof of what happened
  to the Amber Road Company and its thirty travelers.
• Blackroot Warning. A coded note from a duergar liaison warns that the shipment
   contains “stress keys”—chemicals capable of activating dormant biomantic
   changes.
• Temple Appeal. High Priestess Auriel asks the party to locate missing volunteers
   who were promised safe passage and treatment in Frostharbor.

Keyed Locations in Duskmire Crossing
1. Drover’s Rest Inn. A sprawling caravan inn run by Hesta Vane, a stern human
veteran who keeps meticulous arrival books. Her ledger contains three entries
written in the same hand by supposedly different caravan masters.
2. The Cloven Helm Tavern. Teamsters, guards, gamblers, and caravan scouts
gather here. Proprietor Brannik Holt knows which companies hire honest
protection and which accept sealed cargo without questions.
3. Hedgewick Bazaar. A maze of awnings and temporary stalls. Contraband
medicine, foreign spices, replacement wagon parts, and stolen seals can all be
found with enough coin or patience.


4. Willowbell Remedies. Healer Mara Willowbell treats travelers suffering from
heat, exhaustion, and strange graft-like scars. She has hidden two altered survivors
from armed men searching the town.
5. Willowell Tonics. An apothecary and tea shop whose owner unknowingly sold
activation draughts supplied through a Silverreach distributor. Empty crates bear
the same lattice geometry seen in Marrowfen.
6. The Stone Bridge. The settlement’s central crossing. A hollow maintenance
chamber beneath its eastern support has been converted into a temporary surgical
station.
7. East Caravan Yard. Abandoned wagons from DUSK-17 are held under guard.
Their wheel ruts contain glassy green residue that reacts to heat and strong
emotion.
8. South Watch Post. Captain Ilyra Voss commands twenty guards but lacks
authority to search merchant cargo without proof. She can provide riders once the
party exposes the threat.

Part 1: The Driverless Caravan
  THE EMPTY LEAD WAGON
  The lead wagon rolls to a stop at the edge of the yard. Its canvas cover is torn
  from within. Beneath it, crates lie stacked in perfect rows around a single
  wooden chair bolted to the floor. Leather restraints hang open. A thin line of
  green crystal runs from the chair into the wagon frame, pulsing in time with the
  frightened horses’ breathing.


The returning caravan contains six wagons, eight exhausted horses, and no visible
travelers. Searching the wagons reveals bloodless restraint marks, broken water
skins, and personal belongings arranged as if someone catalogued them. A
successful DC 14 Investigation check finds a false floor in the third wagon. Beneath
it is a sedated caravan scout named Toma Reed.
Toma wakes after magical healing or 10 minutes of care. He remembers the
caravan entering a dust storm, guards distributing “heat medicine,” and Doctor
Cael Veyr ordering everyone to keep moving. When panic began, several travelers
changed. Cael separated the transformed from the unaltered and directed the
wagons toward an abandoned toll station in the eastern badlands.

The Wagon Lattice
The chair is an activation cradle. Arcana DC 16 identifies three linked triggers:
elevated body temperature, exhaustion, and fear. A restrained subject exposed to
all three receives a remote lattice pulse. Destroying the cradle is simple, but
preserving it provides proof and advantage on one check to disable Cael’s surgical
engine later.

 DM NOTE. Stress Track. During this chapter, the party may accumulate Stress from
 forced marches, extreme heat, frightening discoveries, or failed group checks. At 3 Stress,
 a character has disadvantage on the first Wisdom saving throw made during the final
 laboratory encounter. A short rest in safety removes 1 Stress; a long rest removes all
 Stress. This is a chapter-specific tension mechanic, not a permanent condition.


Encounter: Changed in the Cargo Yard
While the party investigates, two hidden survivors awaken inside sealed grain
wagons. Use two Lattice-Changed Caravan Guards. They are frightened, confused,
and attack the nearest creatures after hearing Cael’s command through the lattice.
A character can use an action and make a DC 15 Persuasion, Medicine, or Arcana
check to suppress one guard’s aggression until the start of the character’s next
turn. Three total successes calm a guard permanently.


Killing the guards is possible but costs the party potential testimony. Saving at least
one grants advantage on social checks with Captain Voss and Mara Willowbell.


Part 2: A Town Full of Triggers
The party needs to determine where the missing travelers were taken and identify
the courier carrying Frostharbor data. Three investigation threads are available.
Completing any two reveals the abandoned toll station; completing all three grants
one additional advantage in the final assault.

Willowbell’s Patients
  THE HIDDEN SURVIVORS
  Mara opens the cellar door only after every weapon is lowered. Two figures lie
  beneath wet linen sheets. One has a ridge of dark chitin along her spine. The
  other grips the bedframe with fingers that have lengthened into hooked claws.
  Both are awake. Both are crying. “They are still people,” Mara says. “Whatever
  you do next, remember that.”


The survivors, Del Avar and Nessa Vale, were injected by mercenaries before the
dust storm. Medicine DC 15 confirms the changes are incomplete and can be
suppressed. Nessa remembers a symbol painted on Cael’s wagon: a split sun above
a toll arch. Del knows the surgeon used scorpion venom purchased at Hedgewick
Bazaar.
Reward for Compassion. If the party treats the survivors as victims, Mara provides
three doses of Willowbell Cooling Draught. A creature drinking one gains
resistance to fire damage for 1 hour and ignores one level of chapter Stress.

The Bazaar’s False Physician
A merchant named Pel Ordo sold Cael venom, restraints, and desert navigation
charts. He refuses to cooperate unless confronted with Varin records or persuaded
with a DC 16 check. His receipts identify the old Dawnroad Toll Station, twelve
miles east of town. They also reveal that Cael expected a courier named Sahir Pell
to deliver a crystal cylinder to Frostharbor.
If threatened, Pel alerts two disguised Varin agents using Spy stat blocks. Capturing
either agent reveals that Sahir never reached Cael and is hiding somewhere in
Duskmire Crossing.

The Bridge Chamber
A maintenance hatch beneath the eastern bridge support opens into a temporary
operating room. Two cultists and one Varin Biomancer are destroying records
when the party arrives. The chamber contains restraint tables, empty injection


tubes, and a speaking crystal through which Cael repeats the phrase, “Stress
reveals compatibility.”
A successful DC 16 Investigation check finds a route map marking three ambush
sites. The newest mark circles the Dawnroad Toll Station. A ledger lists 18 subjects:
6 dead, 8 transferred, 3 “field viable,” and 1 courier pending.

Finding Sahir Pell
Sahir hides in a rented loft above the Cloven Helm, terrified that both House Varin
and Cael are hunting him. He has the Frostharbor cylinder but refuses to
surrender it unless promised protection. Persuasion DC 15, proof that House Varin
has fallen, or the presence of Captain Voss convinces him.
The cylinder contains partial glacial calibration records, payment codes, and a
message: “Mobile strain confirms activation under sustained stress. Deliver viable
specimens north. Ascension threshold approaches.” Sahir also knows Cael plans to
leave the toll station before dawn.


Optional Duskmire Quests
The Wells Run Bitter
An activation compound has contaminated a public cistern. The party must
identify and neutralize it before evening traffic arrives. Three successes using
Arcana, Nature, Medicine, or Survival (DC 15) prevent a townwide panic. Reward:
250 gp and Captain Voss assigns four guards to the toll-station assault.

The Caravan of Mirrors
A merchant wagon carries polished panels that reflect viewers with subtle
mutations. The mirrors are divination relays recording emotional responses.
Destroying or cleansing them prevents Cael from gaining advance knowledge of
the party’s spellcasters.

The Scorpion Pit
A beast handler keeps oversized desert scorpions beneath the bazaar. Cael paid to
harvest venom, then abandoned one altered specimen. Use a Giant Scorpion with
20 additional hit points and resistance to poison damage. Reward: antitoxin, 200
gp, and enough venom to coat three weapons for one attack each.

The Missing Teamsters
Four teamsters escaped DUSK-17 and hide in a dry river tunnel. They are pursued
by two Human-Scorpion Krasis. Saving them provides a safe route to the toll
station and allows the party to bypass the outer sentries.


A Merchant’s Choice
A wealthy caravan owner offers the party 600 gp to destroy all evidence that his
company transported Varin supplies. Accepting conceals his guilt but provides
access to a reinforced wagon useful during the assault. Exposing him earns public
support and an additional witness against the remaining Varin network.


Part 3: Ambush on the Amber Road
  THE DRY WASH
  The road descends between walls of pale stone. Broken wagon spokes protrude
  from the sand like ribs. No insects move. No wind reaches the wash. Ahead, a
  overturned coach blocks the narrowest point, and something scratches slowly
  against the rock above you.


The twelve-mile journey to the toll station crosses exposed desert and a narrow dry
wash. Unless the party completed The Missing Teamsters, the route is trapped.
Spotting the ambush requires DC 16 Perception or Survival.

Encounter: Caravans of the Changed
Four Human-Scorpion Krasis attack from the cliffs and buried positions. Two begin
hidden beneath loose sand; two cling to the rock walls. Their goal is to capture, not
kill, anyone carrying the Frostharbor cylinder. They retreat toward the toll station
if two are defeated.
Terrain Features. The wash is 20 feet wide with 15-foot cliffs. Loose sand is difficult
terrain in three 10-foot patches. A damaged wagon provides half cover. A hanging
rock shelf can be dropped with a DC 15 Strength check or 10 points of thunder
damage, dealing 3d10 bludgeoning damage in a 10-foot square (DC 15 Dexterity
save for half).
Clue. Each Krasis bears a metal tag stamped with a subject number and one of
three stress symbols: flame, eye, or chain. The tags match categories in Cael’s
ledger: heat, fear, and exhaustion.


Part 4: The Dawnroad Toll Station
The abandoned station is built into a low ridge. Its visible courtyard and stable
conceal a laboratory excavated beneath the tollhouse. Cael’s mercenaries are
preparing two wagons for departure. If the party arrives undetected, they can
sabotage the wagons, free prisoners, or disable the alarm crystal before combat
begins.

Keyed Areas
1. Toll Courtyard
Two veterans and four bandits guard supply wagons. One wagon contains
alchemical triggers; the other holds six restrained travelers. Fire in the courtyard
risks igniting the trigger wagon, creating a 15-foot burst for 4d6 fire damage (DC 15
Dexterity save for half).

2. Stable of Quiet Horses
Eight horses stand perfectly still, each marked with a small lattice scar. Calming
them with Animal Handling DC 14 prevents them from panicking during the
escape. One saddlebag contains 350 gp and House Varin payment seals.

3. Tollmaster’s Office
Cael’s field notes describe the purpose of the trials: determine whether dormant
grafts can be activated by ordinary travel hardship, allowing entire populations to
be altered without permanent laboratories.

4. Prison Gallery
Eight captives are held behind lattice bars. The bars can be disabled with DC 16
Arcana or thieves’ tools, or destroyed (AC 17, 30 hit points). Two captives are
beginning to transform and need magical healing or Medicine DC 15 to remain
conscious.

5. Venom Distillery
Glass coils refine scorpion venom into activation serum. Two Swarms of Insects
and one Chuul guard the chamber. Destroying the still removes Cael’s ability to use
Forced Adaptation twice during the final battle.

6. Observation Theater
Tiered benches overlook the surgical engine. Crystal recordings contain the voices
of House Varin patrons praising the program. Recovering them provides decisive
evidence and reveals that Frostharbor’s core is already synchronized.


7. Surgical Engine
A rotating brass-and-crystal apparatus surrounds three operating tables. Cael Veyr
stands at the central console, flanked by two Human-Scorpion Krasis. Green light
travels through tubes in the floor toward every captive in the complex.

Resting at the Toll Station
 DM NOTE. A short rest is possible only after the courtyard, prison gallery, and alarm
 crystal are secured. If the party retreats for a long rest, Cael departs with the Frostharbor
 data, all surviving captives, and two additional Human-Scorpion Krasis. The final
 encounter then occurs as a moving caravan chase rather than in the surgical chamber.


Part 5: The Arcane Surgeon
  DOCTOR CAEL VEYR
  The man at the console wears a spotless coat over armor stitched from
  overlapping plates of pale chitin. Silver instruments hover around his hands.
  Behind him, three restrained travelers convulse as green lines crawl beneath
  their skin. “You call this cruelty,” he says without looking away from his work.
  “Cruelty is leaving them helpless against the world that is coming.”


Battlefield Features
• Surgical Tables. Three captives are restrained. At initiative count 20, one gains 1
   mutation charge. At 3 charges, that captive becomes a hostile Lattice-Changed
   Guard. A character adjacent to a table can use an action and DC 15 Arcana,
   Medicine, or thieves’ tools check to remove one charge and release the captive.
• Activation Coils. Four coils power Cael’s regenerative harness. Each has AC 15, 18
  hit points, and immunity to poison and psychic damage. Destroying two
  suppresses Cael’s Regeneration; destroying all four also prevents Forced
  Adaptation.
• Venom Channels. A creature pushed into a channel takes 2d6 poison damage and
   must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution save or be poisoned until the end of its
   next turn.
• Observation Windows. Breaking a window creates jagged difficult terrain and
  allows rescued captives to escape into the corridor.

Cael’s Tactics
Cael begins behind the surgical engine, using Flesh Warp and spell attacks while
the two Human-Scorpion Krasis hold the front line. He targets characters with


visible wounds, calling them “high-value stress responses.” If the party destroys
two coils, he abandons restraint and uses Reconstruction on a fallen Krasis. At
fewer than 35 hit points, he attempts to inject himself with the final serum, gaining
20 temporary hit points and a scorpion tail attack until defeated.

Cael’s Confession
If captured or reduced below 15 hit points and offered survival, Cael explains that
Rabu Shin selected Duskmire because caravans reproduce every useful stressor:
heat, thirst, fear, isolation, fatigue, and social dependence. The goal was not to
create individual monsters. It was to prove that whole populations could carry
dormant alterations until the correct conditions activated them.

  THE SURGEON’S CERTAINTY
  “Rabu Shin is not mad. He is ascended. You still imagine him as a man hiding in a
  laboratory. He is the instruction inside every lattice. The command that survives
  the hand that wrote it. Frostharbor is not another experiment. It is the final
  calibration.”


Aftermath
With Cael defeated and the surgical engine disabled, the remaining captives can be
returned to Duskmire Crossing. Captain Voss arrests surviving mercenaries and
closes the east road while healers examine every recent caravan. The party’s
choices determine whether the town responds with compassion, fear, or violence
toward altered survivors.

The Frostharbor Cylinder
The recovered cylinder contains complete shipping coordinates, a glacial access
cipher, and a recorded message from an unknown emissary: “Mobile activation
verified. Thermal extremes confirmed. Deliver all viable strains to the northern
core. Ascension imminent.” The access cipher grants advantage on one Arcana
check made to bypass a Frostharbor lattice seal.

Rewards
• Captain Voss and the merchant companies award 900 gp for rescuing the
   captives and reopening the Amber Road.
• Mara Willowbell provides three potions of greater healing and one restorative
  salve that ends the poisoned or frightened condition when applied as an action.
• Cael’s Silver Surgical Bracer functions as a spellcasting focus. Once per long rest,
   its wearer can cast lesser restoration without expending a spell slot.
• The party receives the Frostharbor Access Cipher and a sealed case of cold-
   weather equipment.
• If Sahir survives, he provides a Varin route map that allows the party to avoid
   one random travel encounter on the journey north.

Advancement
Duskmire Crossing is a bridge chapter and does not automatically grant a level.
Characters remain 9th level unless the Dungeon Master uses experience points or
the party entered the chapter below the recommended level. The information and
equipment recovered here substantially reduce the difficulty of Frostharbor.

If Cael Escapes
• Cael reaches Frostharbor with viable subjects and reinforces the glacial
   laboratory.
• One altered survivor in Duskmire becomes a remote relay, causing a second
  outbreak after the party leaves.
• The Frostharbor core begins with 30 temporary hit points and one additional
   guardian.
• Cael appears during the Frostharbor boss encounter with half hit points and no
   legendary actions.


Transition to Frostharbor
 THE NORTHERN ROAD
 The crystal cylinder opens beneath the cold light of dawn. Lines of green fire
 sketch a coastline buried beneath ice. One point pulses beneath a glacier shelf,
 brighter than every node you have seen before. Across the projection, a final
 message forms: GLACIAL PHASE COMPLETE. The desert wind suddenly feels
 very far away.
